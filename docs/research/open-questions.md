| Q1 | Simulation ticks-per-second of DK2 v1.70? | **STRONGLY INFERRED from the binary and the shipped levels, 2026-09-05: a game second is 4 ticks.** The variable-19 global (`0x006F2590`, 223 readers, the seconds-to-ticks multiplier of Q132) has **two** writers, not the one recorded on 2026-09-04: its dispatch stub, and the **level loader**, which copies a 16-bit field of the loaded LEVEL record into it. The field is the one two bytes after the trigger id; the layout around it (trigger id, this field, a 520-byte block, then the 0x5000-byte message block, then the level flags) matches the LEVEL chunk byte for byte, and it is the field OpenKeeper names `ticksPerSec`. **All 66 shipped level files carry 4 in it** (scanned directly 2026-09-05; every first chunk is LEVEL, sig 220, names cross-checked).
