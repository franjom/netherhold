| Q16 | MAP tile bytes 3 (`flags`, bridge-related) and 4 — meaning? | **BYTE 3 ANSWERED** (2026-09-03, ADR-0031): it marks **a bridge over lava** — 1 everywhere in all 66 shipped maps, 2 on bridge tiles over lava and nowhere else. The old note that it was "bridge-related" was right and it was the whole meaning. Our own evidence for byte 4 was that it is **uniform across an entire level** — every tile of Level6b carries 1 and every tile of Level20 carries 0 — so it is a level property rather than tile data, and it does not correlate with whether the level has water or lava.
