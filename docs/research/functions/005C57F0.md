Table 0x00671F00 slot +0x14, reached by the minimap's 0x005B3340 wrapper.
Bounded instructions establish radius below 1 as one pixel and radius 1 as
four cardinal pixels plus the centre. The larger-radius branch beginning
0x005C5EAF advances an integer circle error and plots symmetric boundary
pixels individually, rather than filling horizontal spans. Clipping and
multiple colour-mode branches exist; their complete contracts were not read.
