A function, not data: every doc cites it as a call taking two coordinate differences, and it lies in cseg (0x00653000 to 0x0066BFFF), the second executable block. The entity turn 0x004B2AE0 uses it for the bearing to its aim, and the melee swing at 0x004990C4 feeds it attacker minus target position, masks the result with 0x7FF and derives the bearing damage bonus; combat-spells.md uses it to face a shot's aim. Bearing.cs reproduces it, stating that its 257 table words are exactly floor(atan(i/256) * 2048 / 2pi), and FightRules passes Bearing.Of into the melee bonus (combat-damage.md section 6, ADR-0153 amended, Q370 answered). Read from the binary and fitted in code; since 2026-10-15 the original itself, run on Unicorn, matches Bearing.Of on about 50000 differences (tests/DK2.Oracle.Tests/OriginalFunctionTests.cs), and its table matches byte for byte (OriginalTableTests.cs), hence CONFIRMED.
