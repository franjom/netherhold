Thiscall on the room, one argument, the corpse. Its one caller is 0x004B1070 at 0x004B1139.

The value is the creature definition's word +0x6BF, reached through corpse +0x129. When the corpse's level byte +0x12D is not 1, it is scaled by the signed word at [0x006E4154] + 0x225 + 2 x level, divided by 100, floored at 0 and capped at 0xFFFE. The value is added to +0x49 first (0x004DD4ED-0x004DD4F9).

It then calls the gate 0x004DDCA0 on the owner (through 0x004E52F0). When the gate passes and the tally is at least the global 0x006F25AC (variable 25, 1000; unsigned JC at 0x004DD515), it stores 0 into +0x49 (0x004DD519) and calls 0x004DD820 once. There is no loop: one burial makes at most one Vampire and the excess is lost. When the gate fails, the tally keeps the sum.

Ours (GraveyardRules, ADR-0151) subtracts 1000 instead and has no gate.
