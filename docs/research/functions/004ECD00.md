Thiscall on the room manager (world +0x9BE), one argument, the room id. It calls Room_OnFreed. It unlinks the room from the manager's in-use list, whose head is manager +0x0E, through the room's +0x1F/+0x21 node (the dumper's RoomManagerNode). It pushes the room onto the free list at manager +0x10 and decrements the count at +0x12. It is the inverse of Room_Allocate (0x004EC490). Callers read: Room_Absorb (the absorbed room) and 0x004ED7B0 (the room losing a tile, at 0x004ED9FA). Read by decompile only, hence LIKELY.
