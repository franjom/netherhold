It first requires the tile to be a Treasury or Heart of the creature's own keeper (`0x004539B0`), else returns 0 without counting an attempt. The take is `0x004F4510` on the tile's room, one pile a tile round the room's ring until the wage `+0x470` is met; nothing taken increments `+0x474` and returns 0. Half the amount goes to the purse `+0x3FB` through `0x0048AE70` (capped at MaxGoldHeld), the full amount leaves the owed wage via `0x0048E810`, and a fully paid wage runs `0x00486F80(4)` to wipe the pay-anger bucket (payday.md §4.4). The room-not-tile reading was corrected in Q435/ADR-0220 and the purse and anger calls are marked CONFIRMED 2026-09-24.
