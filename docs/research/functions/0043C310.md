Builds the tile piece list through 0x0043E320 and submits each entry through the render sink at +0x9E, slots +0xF0 and +0xF4 (wall-rendering.md, piece selection). The sink is the static object 0x00793388 (vtable 0x00670574): whole pieces go to (x + 0.5, y + 0.5), quarters to the quadrant offset, and every vertex is bent by the terrain lattice (terrain-lattice.md).
