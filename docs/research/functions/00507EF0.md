Read 2026-10-09 from the decompile, body confirmed on review. `this` is a keeper's task pool (STRONGLY INFERRED to be the object at player +0x1105 that the capture records, from its +0x4650-+0x467C fields); arguments the kind and a tile pointer. When the kind's count (+0x4678 + kind*2) is nonzero it walks the kind's list from its head (+0x4656 + kind*2), entries of 0x24 bytes, for one whose kind byte (+4) and tile (+0, +2) match; it zeroes +0x23 and the twelve worker words +5..+0x1B, unlinks it (head or the previous entry's +0x1F), decrements the count, links it onto the free list (+0x4654, via +0x21) and increments +0x4650. Callers: the remove-by-id wrapper 0x00508040 (0x00508065; about thirty callers, the task handlers among them), TaskPool_Raise (0x00507CF1, 0x00507D6C: raising kind 1 removes a kind 6 or 16 entry on the tile), 0x00512F10 (0x00513074) and the untag 0x00512A20 (0x00512CE3).
