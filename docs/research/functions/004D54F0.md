ECX is the body (creature +0x124; state 6 calls it through the thunk 0x004D5B20). Returns 0x10000 when +0x10E bit 6 (path_finished) is set or the total +0xFA is zero; otherwise 0x10000 minus remaining/total, where remaining is the octile distance from the body to the current subpath point shifted down 12 (sixteenths of a tile, truncated) plus +0xFE and +0x102. So on the last point with nothing beyond, the result is exactly 1.0 once the body is within 1/16 tile. State 6 resumes on exactly 1.0, which is what ends a job walk (Q490). FollowPathRules.Progress mirrors the formula; whether it truncates the same way is for the builder to check.
