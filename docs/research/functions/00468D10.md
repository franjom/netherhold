Centred on the imp's position, it tests the own tile unfiltered (the chain is called at `0x00468DA0` with no `+0xB` test; that test is only in the ring loops at `0x00468E99` and `0x00468F71`), then rings at each Manhattan distance, four sides walked clockwise corner to corner from a start side given by the chooser `0x00469340` (claims, drop scan, Tier B); a tile is handled only when byte `+0xB` bit `0x20` is clear or it carries a dig or mine task (imp-claim-path.md §2). The start side's local (frame `+0x34`, read at `0x00468E34`) is first written at `0x00468D60` with the result of `0x005558D0(4, world+0x15A0, "…", 0x1E4)` called at `0x00468D54`: the game's random draw modulo 4 on the world's random word (ESP is the frame less 0x10 there, so `[ESP+0x44]` is that slot; the map limits sit at `+0x44`/`+0x48` and the task-pool pointer at `+0x20`). The chooser's result overwrites it at `0x00468DEB` when one is passed, so every scan call draws once, and with no chooser (kinds 6, 7, 8, 16) the side is uniform random. The switch bounds-checks it (`CMP ESI,3; JA` at `0x00468E38`). The 2026-09-30 "stale stack" reading mistook the draw's store for the map-limit local; the earlier `rand(4)` reading was right. Radius is `0x7FFFFFFF` for Tier A, step (a) and Tier B, and `0x2000` (rings 1-3) for the drop scan; it returns at the first accepting tile. The rings are clamped to width−2 and height−2 (`0x00468D43`, `0x00468D46`; read at the high edge only); ours uses `world.Contains`. Ours admits the centre untested in every caller (`RingScanVisits` takes the centre, ADR-0378), so gold under an imp standing in water is offered by Tier B and the drop scan, as in DK2. The diamond shape was found by emulating its bytes and then confirmed from the pointer steps (imp-task-priority.md §2.2, imp-claim-path.md §2). Ours is `DiamondRings`, walked by the claim search (ADR-0285), Tier B (ADR-0291), the drop scan (ADR-0292) and the kinds 6, 7 and 8 searches. Every one draws `rand(4)` on entry; the chooserless ones start on that side, the others overwrite it (ADR-0351, Q526; ADR-0294 had removed the draw on the wrong reading).
