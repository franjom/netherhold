The argument is the bucket index and the receiver is the same int[8] anger block the wrapper 0x00478B80 hands the adder 0x00487030. It adds the bucket's own negation, clamps like the adder, recomputes the mood maximum, and does nothing to the counter when world byte +0x72A1 bit 0 is clear (payday.md section 4.4). Known callers: state 189 with bucket 4 (Pay) when the wage owed reaches zero, state 80 with bucket 2 (Lair) as the bed goes down, and state 118 with bucket 1 (Food) at the food (state-category-hooks.md section 3.2).
