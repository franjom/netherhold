Read whole by decompile. The bit-2 arm (globals 0x006EC9D8 = 0 and (from ^ to) & 4) applies when neither byte is a door. Bit 2 on a plain tile is written on portal centres and, by 0x004EFDE0, on every interior tile of a Prison, Temple or Combat Pit, so a mover enters or leaves those interiors only with option bit 2.
