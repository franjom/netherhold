Clears bit 11 of the dword at +0x3EA (AND DH,0xF7 at 0x0048E14D) and ORs in (arg & 1) << 11 (store at 0x0048E155). The bit is the one 0x0048E110 reads. Its three callers: the trigger dispatch 0x00518C59 (Flag bit 2), the party spawn 0x0051A577 (member Flags2 bit 2) and the placed-hero loader 0x00520857 (record +0x53 bit 2).
