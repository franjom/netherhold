Not a Ghidra function: the previous one returns at 0x00497DE2 and this entry opens with its own SEH prologue; the only reference is its install in the action table at 0x004952CF (record 11). Fastcall on the creature. The tile is the action point (+0x38D/+0x391, 12.12). Both arms require the tile's room word (+2 & 0xFFF) and the room definition's flag byte +0x43D bit 0x80 (Buildable); without it the action ends with 0, without a room it ends with 1 or 0 by the +0xF1 tail. The arm is chosen by the creature's owner (+0x28) against the Good player [0x006F254C]:
- Good: AddExperience, then delta = [0x006F2578] (AttackRoomHealth, -1500 / 4) times 10 (LEA x5, SHL 1), no rate; while the tile is still a room afterwards it posts message 0x22 to the tile's owner (0x004C4600) and returns 0; once it is not, it compares the owner's room count of that type (0x004C5FD0) before and after and posts 0x24 (it was the last) or 0x25.
- Anyone else: delta = AttackRoomHealth times the rate (+0x3AF plus +0x3B3, 12.12), no experience and no messages.
The delta goes to the tile through 0x004508D0 (world +0x9DA, the tile at (x, y), then the tile applier 0x00452F60 with the creature's owner) between 0x004F0840 and 0x004F09B0 on the same rectangle. So it damages tile health, never room health (+0x35): a room tile falls when its own 10000 is gone and the applier unmakes it (0x004C5DB0 with no refund). After the tile stops being a room, both arms raise a world event 0x146 for the creature (vtable +0x38C). Started only from states 180, 181 and 267 (creature-action-table.md section 4; 0x0045845B, 0x004585AD, 0x00458661).
