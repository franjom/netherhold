Read whole by decompilation and disassembly (2026-10-08). The room is the neighbour tile's (tile word +2 & 0xFFF through 0x004559B0); the definition pointer is room +0x19, and its byte +0x43E bit 0 is the SpecialWalls flag (bit 8 of the flags word at +0x43D, whose bits 9-11 the loader reads as the torch colours at 0x004EBCD5). The loop (0x004E450F-0x004E455B) takes the next holder from the kicked creature's own link +0x238, which the kick's release (0x004E5E00, at 0x004E5FA5/0x004E5FB1) has zeroed, so it ends after the first holder whose kick is taken: the head, the latest claimant (a claim links at the head). The slot handlers 0x004F1FB0, 0x004F1F10, 0x004E17E0 and 0x004E1830 re-read the head and reach every holder. Entity 0 is taken to be empty (the table at 0x006EE540 is not readable statically). The switch on room +0x18 has arms for types 6, 7, 10, 12 and 15 only; the Prison (11) has SpecialWalls and no arm, the Temple lacks the flag. The caller loop at 0x004501D6 runs on every Tile_SetTerrain whatever the new terrain, over the four entries of 0x006BC350, skipping a neighbour off the map or with no room. Ours marks the holder with the highest claim serial not already marked (SlabChain.KickNeighbours, CreatureState.HeldPlaceSerial) and it takes the kick at its next turn (RoomEvictRules, TortureSystem); a head whose kick DK2's transition table refuses, after which DK2 tries the next, is not modelled.
