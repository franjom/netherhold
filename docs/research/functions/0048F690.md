Whole body read 2026-11-10 in disassembly, 0x0048F690..0x0048F92C. The margin is m = (definition +0x63A >> 1) + 0x80, +0x63A being the footprint radius (file 0xEA0). Four flags: y within m of the low edge, y within m of the high edge, x within m of the low edge, x within m of the high edge (m against 0x1000 minus m). For each side whose flag is set it tests the neighbour tile on that side with 0x00455CE0(tile, mode), the mode coming from 0x00455CA0 (the own tile); on the edge it also tests the adjacent diagonals, and a side counts blocked if the orthogonal neighbour is, else if the diagonal on the other axis' side is. Blocked on the y-low side: y = tileY * 0x1000 + m; y-high: tileY * 0x1000 + 0x1000 - m; x-low: tileX * 0x1000 + m; x-high: tileX * 0x1000 + 0x1000 - m. A side not blocked leaves its coordinate unchanged. It pushes to exactly m from a blocked edge when already within m of it; it is not a general clamp. Callers: 0x004595E9 and 0x004595FF (Creature_EnterDeathFall), 0x00459759 and 0x0045976F (Creature_EnterAlternateDeathFall), 0x004810A1 (state 209's starter). Not built (ADR-0732 builds the picks, not this position).
