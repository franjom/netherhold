Callers pass the definition's units byte +0x672 (1 for every shipped creature); every room finder a job handler uses asks it, and the claim 0x004E5A70 asks again. For a Lair the capacity is the tile count, so the lair finder 0x004E0470's call with 1 is the free-bed test (lair-acquisition.md §4).
