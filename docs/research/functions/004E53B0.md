It takes (room, units) and passes while the word UsedCapacity at room+0x6B plus units is at most TotalCapacity at room+0x67; Library (6) must also pass 0x004E1AC0, Workshop (10) 0x004F6140, and a Guard Room (9) adds the owner's creatures on guard duty there. Callers pass the definition's units byte +0x672 (1 for every shipped creature); every room finder a job handler uses asks it, and the claim 0x004E5A70 asks again. For a Lair the capacity is the tile count, so the lair finder 0x004E0470's call with 1 is the free-bed test (lair-acquisition.md §4).
