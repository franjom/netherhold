A thiscall on the target with three stack arguments (delta, source, mode); mode 0 is ordinary, and a non-zero mode lets the dying countdown pass the early return for a CreatureDying creature. It first writes the source to thing +0x36 for every class; objects take the delta flat (an Arch Pillar forwards to the heart, win-and-defeat.md §3.1), traps too. For a creature with a negative delta it refuses dying, OnlyAttackableByHorny, IsHorny and InHand targets, then scales by a multiplier in 128ths (frozen 160, armoured or possessed -64, owner byte +0x1095 -8 each, stunned +128, pit fighter per variable 239). At health <= 0 with GenerateDeadBody it sets health to CreatureDyingStateDurationSeconds x ticks and body bit 21; the death block also reaches vampire reincarnation 0x0048FFC0 (Q111). About 40 callers: the swing, shots, spells, prison, torture, needs and the heart among them. Built across StatusEffectRules (the creature damage branch) and CreatureDeathRules (the death block, Q303).
