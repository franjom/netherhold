Read from 0x004EC650 to 0x004EC800: the neighbour scan over table 0x006BC350 (N, E, S, W) picks the first neighbour room by the tile room word (+2 & 0xFFF) unless one was passed in (+0x16/+0x1A); the tile is marked in the map (0x00450770) and linked after LastSlab (+0x2F/+0x30), Size is incremented, and 0x004E5300 gets StartingHealth (terrain +0x1AB) with the room's own PlayerId (+0x1D) as the player argument. Since the delta is positive the applier clamps at MaxHealth times the new size. The loop that follows calls Room_Absorb (0x004ECB70) at 0x004EC86C for each later neighbour of a different room. Its caller is 0x004ED320. The append is one consecutive run (2026-10-04): the old tail tile's next link (0x004EC791/0x004EC793), LastSlab set to the new tile (0x004EC79A/0x004EC79D) and the word Size incremented (0x004EC7A0). Size and LastSlab are never out of step, and the room keeps its type overlay (Q291, Q522). The loop's body after 0x004EC800 is not read, hence LIKELY. SlabChain.Added and RoomHealthRules mirror it.
