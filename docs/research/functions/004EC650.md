Read from 0x004EC650 to 0x004EC800: the neighbour scan over table 0x006BC350 (N, E, S, W) picks the first neighbour room by the tile room word (+2 & 0xFFF) unless one was passed in (+0x16/+0x1A); the tile is marked in the map (0x00450770) and linked after LastSlab (+0x2F/+0x30), Size is incremented, and 0x004E5300 gets StartingHealth (terrain +0x1AB) with the room's own PlayerId (+0x1D) as the player argument. Since the delta is positive the applier clamps at MaxHealth times the new size. The loop that follows calls Room_Absorb (0x004ECB70) at 0x004EC86C for each later neighbour of a different room. Its caller is 0x004ED320. The loop's body after 0x004EC800 is not read, hence LIKELY. SlabChain.Added and RoomHealthRules mirror it.
