The near-destination flag (+0xF1 bit 0) is computed against the dwords at +0xE3/+0xE7 (travel-states.md §5: under 7's layout a meaningless value). Then Body_PathProgress (0x004D54F0, via 0x004D5B20); on exactly 0x10000 it sets the body's velocity to zero (0x004D59A0 with its own position), reads the tile's room (word +2 & 0xFFF, Tile_Room) and, when the room's id (+4) equals +0xE3 and its type (+0x18) equals +0xE5, calls Creature_StopMoving, SetState(+0xDF) and +0xDF := 1, returning the SetState result.
