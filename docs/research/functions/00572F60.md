The angle is trunc(atan2(dx, dy) * 2^24 / (2 pi)) masked to 24 bits, dx and dy being the tile centre minus the camera position (constants at 0x0066F9A0 pi, 0x0066F9D8 2^24, 0x0066F9E0 0.5); the half width is trunc(2^21 / distance); both ends are masked unless exactly 2^24, ordered, and an arc wider than 2^23 is split into [0, lo] and [hi, 2^24] and tested through 0x005733D0.
