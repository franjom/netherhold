Reached through the creature cleanup slot +0x30 (0x0048AF80) once health is below 1. It increments the killer keeper's per-victim-owner tally at player+0x19E indexed by the victim owner's Number, and, unless creature+0x3E6 bit 0x40 is set, the victim owner's creatures-lost dword at player+0x173 (player-object.md; condition type 22 reads both, Q276). Inside it the gold spill at 0x0048814B, the carried object and dragged creature releases, and the owner-list unlink at 0x00488468 are recorded (imp-mana-removal.md, creature-ownership.md), and the corpse gate tests definition bit 0x8000 with runtime bit 0x20. It also fires the Killed trigger event (trigger-walkers.md). The counter write is read instruction by instruction; the other effects are marked strongly inferred, so LIKELY.
