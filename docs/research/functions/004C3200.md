The category-5 update 0x0047B190 passes it the level-scaled ManufacturePerSecond every turn. It adds to the word at +0x217; if the 128-entry order ring at +0x219 is non-empty and its head cost is covered (0x00506B50), it finds the nearest staffed Workshop (0x004C3B50), makes the crate through 0x004F5240, zeroes the points and pops the order (state-category-hooks.md §3.4). It has the same casino bonus gate on player +0x20D as the research adder (at 0x004C320B), which never opens because nothing writes that field non-zero (Q217).
