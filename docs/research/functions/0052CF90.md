The complete body dispatches among RGB565, RGB555 and other channel-mask/depth paths or returns -1 on unsupported inputs. Its arguments are (result, destination buffer, x, y, source image, source rectangle or null, flags); the front end draws its panel strips and scroll-bar frames with it (0x0053A580, 0x00551BA0). Its 0xF0 constant is an image mask, not a pointer event.
