Picks the free region record with the smallest count at `0x6E96C8` (3 bytes each: word count, in-use byte; a zero count is taken at once, else `0x004C9080` frees the smallest), marks it in use, re-labels the seed triangle (decrementing its old region's count) and pushes it. Each popped triangle's neighbours at `+6`, `+8` and `+0xA` are visited when not -1, not solid (bit 3 of `+0xC`) and not already in the region; each is re-labelled, counted and pushed. `0x004CCB30` is called on each triangle taken. The queue is `0x6EB668` with head `0x6E86E8`, tail `0x6E5470`, depth `0x6E8EA8` and a high-water mark `0x6ECA48`.

Because the walk takes only the three neighbour words, two floor regions that touch at a single vertex (two tiles diagonal to each other with both orthogonal neighbours solid) receive different ids, and 0x004C9400 then refuses a route between them. STRONGLY INFERRED: it reads the words as edge neighbours, as the flip and the search do; a mesh dump holding a corner-only pair would settle it. That is the binary half of Q28 (ADR-0339). Ours keeps no region ids; a failed `MeshRouter` search, which likewise crosses edges only, stands in (ADR-0157).
