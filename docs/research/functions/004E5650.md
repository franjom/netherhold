In order: decrement a count on the old owner, relink the room into the new owner's list, write the owner into room+0x1D, increment the count on the new owner, reskin, walk the existing slab links updating tile ownership (without relinking the slabs), eject every object, call slot +0x0d (for a portal 0x004DBBC0, the tally reset), credit the new owner with a room count (three types excluded) and a value by tiles, and post messages 32 and 35 to the new and old owner (33 instead of 32 when the old owner is neutral). It also explores the local player's tiles of the room and redraws the minimap. Callers include the room damage path via 0x004E5300, trigger dispatch 0x00518AD0 and both defeat arms 0x004DAA10 and 0x004DAE40. Read from the binary (portals-interval.md; room-slab-chain.md §10 grades the caller picture STRONGLY INFERRED), hence LIKELY. Mirrored in part: DefeatRules hands rooms to neutral by retagging tiles and TileWork fires the portal reset when a portal tile changes hands; the object ejection is not built; the messages are (RoomHealthRules.PostTransfer, Data2 our first slab). It does not write the room's Health (+0x35): StoreAt over every block finds that word written only by Room_Construct, the applier 0x004E5300 and Room_SetHealth, so a room handed over here keeps its damage, and a conversion's reset to StartingHealth times Size is the applier's own step after this call.
