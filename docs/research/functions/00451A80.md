Arguments x, y, width, height and a flag; the rectangle is clamped like `0x004519F0`. A tile with neither the explored bit (`+8` & `0x2000`) nor the scanned bit (`+0xB` & `0x40`) is always rewritten: class `0x0D` when the flag is set, otherwise `0x00451C90`. Explored or scanned tiles are rewritten only on paths that test terrain flags `+0x17B` bit `0x08` and `0x01000000` and, when tile `+0xB` bit `0x01` (the secret-door flag, see `0x00450FF0`) is set, compare the `+8` word's low 12 bits with world slot `+0x264` (the local player), skipping the rewrite when equal. The meaning of terrain bit `0x08` is not identified here. Its only direct caller is `0x00508D2F`, a vtable method (data xref `0x0066E5E0`) whose virtual callers are unread. Class `0x0D` is the dig-tag highlight class (tag-highlight.md §1); this writes it directly, without the view-grid test at the head of `0x00451C90`. Nothing here gives a room colour to an unexplored, unscanned tile (Q197).
