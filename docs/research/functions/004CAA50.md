Arguments (x, y) in tiles. Locates (x << 8, y << 8) with 0x004C98B0 and returns if any of the three corners equals (x, y). For edges 0, 1, 2 in turn it tests the 64-bit orientation of the point against the edge's two endpoints; the first edge with a zero result takes the on-edge path: a point slot is popped from the list at 0x006A11D8 (the link is the slot's x word; -1 means empty, then the high-water mark 0x006ECA34 is used and bumped; the count 0x006ECA38 rises), its y word is zeroed and then x, y written; u is the neighbour across that edge and j its back slot; the new triangle a = 0x004CA820(t, k, x, y, p) and b = 0x004CA820(u, j, x, y, p) are made in that order, then t's neighbour k := b and u's neighbour j := a. Otherwise 0x004CA500(t, x, y) splits the interior. No in-circle test, no legalisation.
