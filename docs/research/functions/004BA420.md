Takes a signed amount, a flag and a tile. It adds to `player+0x7E` at `0x004BA61D` and subtracts at `0x004BA63F`, so the total tracks every deposit and withdrawal between the player tick's resyncs. With no tile it only adjusts `+0x7E` (the PLAYERS loader `0x0051F0B0` pays starting gold this way); training charges `-cost` at the creature's tile (`0x0047B3CA`), and blueprint placement and the Casino take also go through it. A positive amount with flag 0 and a tile is spread over the Treasuries and unruined Heart until a pass stores nothing (`0x004F4090` returns what a room did not take); any left over posts 41 with no Treasury or 42 with one when the Treasury availability row is not 1, then drops as a floor pile. The room sale `0x004C5DB0` is such a caller. Read from the binary; the docs agree. Our gold handling is not one mirrored routine, so READ.
