Takes a signed amount, a flag and a tile. It adds to `player+0x7E` at `0x004BA61D` and subtracts at `0x004BA63F`, so the total tracks every deposit and withdrawal between the player tick's resyncs. With no tile it only adjusts `+0x7E` (the PLAYERS loader `0x0051F0B0` pays starting gold this way); training charges `-cost` at the creature's tile (`0x0047B3CA`), and blueprint placement, the Casino take and the keeper AI's Treasury messages (41/42) also go through it. Read from the binary; the docs agree. Our gold handling is not one mirrored routine, so READ.
