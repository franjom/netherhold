role: Turns around a vertex, from a triangle and the vertex's slot in it, across the neighbour words and the slot tables until the sought point lies strictly inside the edge arriving at the vertex and on or inside the edge leaving it; leaves the triangle and slot in the pointers and returns next(slot).

Arguments (x, y, &triangle, &slot), the point in 1/256 tile. First `a = cross(v - p, prev(slot) - p) > 0`; then in a loop `b = cross(v - p, next(slot) - p) > 0`, breaking when `a && !b`; otherwise it crosses the neighbour across edge `slot` (the edge leaving the vertex), returning -1 with the pointers untouched at the hull, finds the vertex's slot there as next(back slot), sets `a = b` and goes on. Called by the locate walk 0x004C98B0 in its switch cases 3, 5, 6 and 7, where the point lies beyond two edges; by the endpoint locate 0x004C9DF0; and by the constraint insertion 0x004CBD10, which uses the returned next(slot) as the edge opposite the vertex. Ours is `NavMesh.TurnFan` / `FanToward` (navigation-mesh-and-search.md §2.3), written for our winding: strictly inside the leaving edge, on or inside the arriving one, crossing the arriving edge.
