Whole body read. Target is narrowed to signedshort, stored at+0xC; step is signedshort((target-current)/duration), with current at+4, step at+0x14 and duration word+0x1C. An equal target writes step 0; a zero quotient returns without writing +0x14, so the old step stays (only +0x1C is zeroed). No interpolation happens in this setter.
