Table 0x00671F00 slot +0x18, reached by the minimap's 0x005B33C0 wrapper.
Radius below 1 writes one pixel; radius 1 writes the four cardinal pixels
and centre. For larger radii, instructions at 0x005C816E onward submit a
centre span of width 2*r+1 and further spans through virtual +0x20, whose
target 0x005C53E0 writes consecutive pixels. Complete colour-mode branches
and exact boundary rasterization were not established by this bounded pass.
