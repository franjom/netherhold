Thiscall on the player. It walks the room list (head +0x36, next +0x08) and sums the word TotalCapacity (+0x67) of every type-14 room, noting whether there was one. It walks the creature list (head +0x06, next +0x10) and counts creatures whose definition (+0x370) has +0x686 bit 23 (CanBeResurrected) and whose vtable +0x28 returns 0. Reading +0x28 as "dead or dying", from its use at 0x004B30FD in the health routine, is STRONGLY INFERRED. When the count is at least the sum, it posts Message_Post(42, player, 14, 0, 1) if a Graveyard exists, and returns 0. Otherwise it returns 1.

With no Graveyard the sum is 0, so it always fails. Callers: Graveyard_BuryCorpse (0x004DD509), which then raises no Vampire; Vampire_Reincarnate (0x0048FFFB), which then does not reincarnate; and the corpse selections that post 42 (Q544). Not built.
