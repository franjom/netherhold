Loops over the four offsets of `0x006BC350` (four iterations to `0x006BC374`), not eight. For each neighbour in the map: clears its bit when the tile is owned (not neutral), its terrain flag bit 1 is clear and the neighbour is not flag 0x100/0x80; never sets a neighbour's bit. Sets the tile's own bit unless something in it or the four neighbours is owned, it is not flag 0x100/0x80 and something is passable. Called by the tile setter `0x0044FC40` and the change notifier `0x00455030`. The ring scan `0x00468D10` skips tiles with the bit set unless they carry a dig or mine task (imp-claim-path.md §2; Q487). Not built: our claim gates repeat the adjacency test it anticipates.
