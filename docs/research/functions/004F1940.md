Called as 0x004F1940(room, creature) from the handler 0x0046EAB0 on every update of 76 while the cell is a Temple floor slot and the unsigned height above the floor is below 0x1000. If the room's word +0x429 is nonzero it requests effect 0x62 through the effect service (+0x38C) at the creature's x and y with z 0x1000, and sound 0xE2 in the creature's category (+0x6E3). It is not the sacrifice. The meaning of +0x429 is unknown. Not built.
