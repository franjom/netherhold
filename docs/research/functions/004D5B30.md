The reset stores radius 0x3333 (0.2 in 16.16) at +0xDC, max_force 0x18000 (1.5) at +0xDE and max_speed 0x6666 (0.4) at +0xE2; no creature-record field feeds them, so separation does not vary with creature size. It overwrites the constructor's earlier max_force 0x10000 and max_speed 0x50000, which never reach the mover.
