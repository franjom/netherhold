Point (i, j, k) = (x + i/2, y + j/2, z0 + k*s + o) with s = 1/2, o = 0, or s = 1/4, o = 0.75 when all three flags are zero. 0x00759AE8 is the possession-style camera flag (modes 2, 10, 11), 0x0075AEAF the 3D front end's; that the keeper's view therefore draws walls at half height above the floor is STRONGLY INFERRED in the research note.
