Called only from `0x00489076`, on a tick the tile coordinates changed, for a creature whose definition has `+0x686` bit 0, whose owner's player byte `+0x10FC` is at least 3 and whose effective category is not 22. Inside: nothing carried (`+0x3C3`, `+0x3C5`), effective category 3 (`0x00478050`), the imp's tile more than two tiles from `+0x8C/+0x90` on either axis, and dword 6 of the kind record at `0x006986A0` set for the current kind. Then `0x004687C0(14)` on the tile; on success the walk's resume is set to 10 (`0x00478080`). The dispatcher `0x00468520` has no case 14, so this is the only way a kind-14 task is taken (imp-claim-path.md §3, §5).

Nothing limits it to a deposit walk. Level 1 seeds 3, 4 and 5 take it on a job walk to a dig (seed 5: imp 452, walking to (14,32), steps onto (10,28) at tick 142 and holds the claim at 143 without passing 10); seeds 2 and 7 on a deposit walk; 2 to 4 a run before tick 700. Built on `NeedRules.ResolvedCategory == WorkerJob` (the state, or the state a walk resumes, as `0x00478050` gives it) with no object or creature carried, tried before the walk-over pick-up as `0x0048904E` does (ADR-0378), replacing ADR-0303's job, fetch and deposit activities. A wander walk resumes idle and does not claim.
