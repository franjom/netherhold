Id at record +0x10, value at +0x14. Id 0xAE stores raw to 0x006F294B. Any other value is IDIV'd by [0x006F2590] (variable 19, 4), then id - 4 indexes the byte table 0x0051CCD8 (173 entries) into the jump table 0x0051CC94. Ids 4-16 go to 0x006F2554-0x006F2580 in the order of the table (14 ConvertRoomHealth 0x006F2574, 15 AttackRoomHealth 0x006F2578, 16 RepairRoomHealth 0x006F2580; 9 and 10 land at 0x006F2584 and 0x006F2588); **136 ConvertNeutralRoomHealth goes to 0x006F257C**; 175 and 176 to 0x006F294F and 0x006F2953; everything else, id 17 included, to the no-op 0x0051CC8C. Id 17 is the research list (0x0051DB00) and is not a rate. So the reader 0x00497C89 in action 10 reads variable 136.
