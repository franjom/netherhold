Not a Ghidra function (its bytes sit between 0x00499300 and 0x004997FE, right after Combat_MeleeSwing); read from the disassembly for the sounds 2026-11-02 and again for the effects 2026-11-11.

Gate. It needs a tile with a nonzero room word (+2 low 12 bits) and the device chooser 0x004F2180 answering 1, else it falls to 0x0049977A (the action's end test, 0x0048ECE0). The tile at the creature's own point (EDI, 0x00499308..0x0049931B) is the one whose owner is compared: [EDI+8] & 0xFFF against the creature's owner [ESI+0x28] (0x00499362..0x00499372). When they differ it posts message 0x38 to the creature's owner (0x00499384). The device tile (EBP, from the point at 0x0049935C) is compared only afterwards, at 0x00499391 (CMP EBP,EDI); when the two tiles differ it also falls to 0x0049977A.

Then the cell is tested for a floor slot (0x00454110). Non-floor-slot tile (the wall wheel): the device must be type 8 (0x004993AC), else it returns. The cell is searched for an object of type 0x3F (0x004537E0) and then 0x3E:
- Water trough (0x3F): device sound `0xFB` (device definition +0x10E, at the device's +0x1A, 0x00499404, behind `CMP [0x00759ADC],1`); then, when the victim's `+0x68` is in [0x3A0, 0x460) and the game turn is a multiple of ticks-per-second (0x006F2590), view effect 0x117 (279) through world slot +0x38C at the device's point with the victim's owner (0x0049946B, NOT behind the global); then the victim's sound `0x31C` (0x00499767 by the tail at 0x0049948C, behind the global).
- Coals trough (0x3E): device sound `0x31B` (0x004994D7, behind the global); the same victim and turn condition, then view effect 0x112 (274) (0x0049953E, NOT behind the global); then the victim's sound `0x31B` (tail from 0x0049955F, behind the global).
- Neither trough: device sound `0xFB` (0x00499593, behind the global), then the victim's `0xCF` by the tail from 0x00499748.
So the water arm and the coals arm differ in object, effect and both sounds. The effect is requested on each turn that is a multiple of ticks-a-second (once per game second; the "once a second" meaning is inferred from the modulus) while the victim's +0x68 is in [0x3A0, 0x460) (meaning unread), whether or not the global is 1. When the range test or the whole-second test fails, the handler jumps to its return at 0x004997F2 and skips the victim's trough sound as well.

Floor-slot tile: a type 8 device sets process state 0x1E (0x0049EF60, 0x00499736) and plays the victim's `0xCF` (the tail at 0x00499748, behind the global at 0x0049973B). A type 0x3D device (the chair) works on the action's age: every 6 x ticks-a-second turns it sets the device's +0x92 bit 0 and draws the victim's byte +0x4CD (the world's second random stream, world +0x15A0, 0x005558D0, source line 0x84A, at 0x004996B0), and 3 x ticks-a-second turns after that it clears the bit and sets +0x4CD to 0xFF (0x004996FF). When +0x4CD is 0xFF and the global is 1 at the set phase, it plays the device's `0xFB` at 0x00499634 and the victim's `0x156` at 0x0049965D (reads of the global at 0x00499605 and 0x00499663).

Global 0x00759ADC has no direct writer (open-questions.md, Q635): at 0 none of the sounds is asked, but the trough effects still are. Not mirrored: no code of ours implements this handler (torture-information.md and TortureSystem are the category hook 0x0047B590); ours raises neither trough effect (openkeeper-pr-audit-1000-1110.md, PR 1041).
