Four stack arguments (position, resume, options, noTeleport; `RET 0x10`) and 45 call sites; every imp task handler walks through it, passing the job's entry state as the resume (travel-state-call-sites.md §3, imp-task-priority.md §3). It scans ability slots 0..2; a slot counts when its entry's level byte is at most the creature's (`+0x403`), its id is non-zero and `0x0048C800` says it is ready. A slot whose record has `+0x27 & 0x20` ends the loop (the teleport). One with `+0x27 & 0x40` is cast on the spot: its stamp `+0x41B + 4*slot` = now, `0x0048CAD0(def+0x1F, def+0x23)`, and `+0x4DD` = `def+0x1F` when that is above 100. The cast runs whatever `noTeleport` says, and `0x0048CAD0` does nothing while a speed effect runs; for the Imp it is Imp Haste (travel-state-call-sites.md §3, Q401). With a teleport slot selected, `noTeleport` 0, the target more than two tiles away and `0x00471630` accepting, it saves the resume at `+0xDF`, starts action 60 and enters state 51 (teleporting), resumed from 52; otherwise it calls `0x00471890(position, resume, options)`.
