Reads room +0x0A, calls state141 on its head and reloads +0x0A until empty. There are no countdown, prior-standing count or fight-stamp writes. Consequently an ownership change preserves the pit runtime on the surviving room object.

Mirrored as ReleaseAttached at room free, absorption and owner-change paths, repeatedly selecting the latest attached claimant until no holder remains. It does not perform the map-fighter clearing pass, which belongs only to the after-rebuild callback.

Suspected DK2 defect: DK2-041. The loop retries the same attached head when SetState141 refuses without unlinking it (0x004D8000..0x004D8022); whether gameplay can leave such a refusing holder attached is not established.
