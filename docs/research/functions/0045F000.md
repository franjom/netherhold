State 8 (`0x004710E6`) is its one caller, for `CreatureRetreatingCounter` (`+0x53D`); the same two words are tested inline by the Blocker, Support and Flanker selectors (their exclusion), spFighting (`0x0045F960`), `0x0045A7D6` (MayLookForFight) and `0x0045E0D0`.
