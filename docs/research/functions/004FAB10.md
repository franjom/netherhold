Receiver ECX is the keeper struct. Walks the owner's creature list (player +0x06 head, creature +0x10 next): a creature whose definition flag bit 0 (worker) is clear counts, and counts again as under-levelled when its level +0x403 is below the four-bit field at +0x4B2 bits 19 to 22. Returns 0 when the count is at most IAMinimumCreatures (+0x4D7). When the under-levelled count is at most that level field and strictly below the count, every under-levelled non-worker gets one call of 0x0048B120 (the ordinary level-up) before the final walk, which returns 0 at the first non-worker still below the level and 1 otherwise. No health, hand, captive or state test. Callers: the attack planner 0x004FAEF0, the tick's training step at 0x00500C20 (after random(100) < 50 with allowance left) and the Train job predicate 0x00502EF0, so the grant runs from three sites.
