cdecl (record*, cap), the GateWalk_Collapse (0x004CFC50) record layout. Octile distances of A and B to the crossing; both under cap >> 1 returns. A under (cap + 2) >> 1 sets the selector to 0 and calls GateWalk_Collapse; else B under it sets 1 and calls it. Otherwise the unit direction is the Q14 divide of the shifted difference by Math_Isqrt(sum of Q14 squares) << 7, each component shifted down 6 (zero for a zero length), scaled by cap >> 9. The crossing minus that becomes A if it lies in the box of A and B; then the crossing plus that becomes B if it lies in the box of the (possibly moved) A and B. Read whole (0x004CFDA0-0x004CFFC2). Calls only 0x004CFC50 and 0x0065FAE0; no globals.
