Read through 0x004435E0; the room wall chooser and the plain-terrain builder draw such a tile as wall. Bit 15 is neither open nor closed: 0x004488A0 writes it from its record's flag (EDX, loaded from the stack slot at 0x00448945 and unchanged on both arms to the push at 0x004489E7), which that function forces to zero when the record's player is the viewer. So the bit is set for a viewer the door is hidden from and never for its owner. Ours derives it as the tile's secret-door marker and a door owner other than the viewer (`WorldView.IsHiddenSecretDoor`); that every sender's flag equals the marker is inferred from the reveal arm of 0x004A55F0, which clears the marker and posts flag 0, and from 0x004A6E10, which tests the marker itself.
