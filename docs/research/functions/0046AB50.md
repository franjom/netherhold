Refuses a tile the imp owns, terrain flags 0x81 or 0x100, a door (world slot `+0x21C`), an owner that is neither neutral nor allowed by `0x004BF3D0`, and one with no orthogonal own floor neighbour (`0x00453B30`). Then finds or raises the kind-3 task (`0x00508200`, `0x00507BB0`) and writes its subkind (`0x005080E0`): 0 and state 15 when `0x00453FC0` passes, 2 and state 19 for enemy floor (`0x00454000`), 1 and state 17 for an enemy room (`0x00454040`). A free slot `[0][0]`: leave hook `0x004647D0`, walk `0x004651A0` to the tile centre. A held one: the holder must be live, a creature with a state machine, not in 51/52 and pass `0x00477310(10)`, else refused; the taker needs a squared distance to the tile centre strictly below the holder's, both from where they stand; then the leave hook, the walk, and only if the walk started `0x0048AD30(10)` on the holder. On success `+0x8A` id, `+0x8C/+0x90` tile, `+0x94/+0x96` 0, the slot takes the imp's id, `+0x98` 3 (imp-claim-path.md §4).
