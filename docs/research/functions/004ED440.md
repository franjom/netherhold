Thiscall on world +0x9BE, RET 0x10; its only caller is Player_SellRoomTile (0x004C5DB0). On a water tile (terrain flag 0x80) it first posts a bridge command around it. It saves the type-specific words the pieces must keep (a Wooden Bridge's +0x49, a Prison's +0x59/+0x5D, a Casino's +0x53), then the share: pct = ((Health << 12) / (MaxHealth x old Size)) x 100 >> 12, through the 12.12 divide 0x00441990 and 0x004E6C60. 0x004ED7B0 removes the tile and returns the rebuilt pieces in a 32-slot buffer (it re-inserts the remaining tiles through 0x004ED320, which writes start healths that this then overwrites). Each piece gets Room_SetHealth(max(1, MaxHealth x Size x pct / 100)) (an unsigned divide by 100), and its saved words. Then, per piece: 0x004E6BC0, 0x004ECEB0, 0x004EF5E0, 0x004EDFC0; a redraw of every slab unless the type is 8, 17 or 22 (the bridges); and Room_OnTilesChanged (0x004E4480).
