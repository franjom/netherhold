name: NavMesh_InsertRectangle
role: Inserts one rectangle of one navigation byte into the mesh: its four corners as points, its four sides as constraints, then the byte stamped over the triangles inside (0x004CC9B0).
confidence: LIKELY
status: READ
evidence: docs/research/navigation-partial-rebuild.md

Normalises the corners to min/max, inserts them in the order (x0,y0), (x0,y1), (x1,y1), (x1,y0), then the constraints (x0,y0)→(x0,y1)→(x1,y1)→(x1,y0)→(x0,y0) through 0x004CBD10, then stamps through 0x004CC9B0: the corner triangles first, then a sweep of every slot (0x004CC850) until the stamped area equals the rectangle's. Called inside 0x004CE770 by the whole-map path for each rectangle the scanner (0x004CE570) yields, and by the partial path for a single tile or a uniform rectangle under 4x4. Ours (`NavMesh.InsertRectangle`) inserts the corners as (x0,y0), (x1,y0), (x1,y1), (x0,y1) with constraints a→b→c→d→a and stamps by a flood of the triangles inside: the same triangles, but the point splits happen in another order, so the slots allocated differ from the original's (a residual of ADR-0461, Q591).
