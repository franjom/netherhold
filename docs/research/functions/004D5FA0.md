Not a Ghidra function (forced at its address). Slot +0x15 of record 8 in the room-type table. It gates on `(turn + room+4) % (TicksPerSecond * 5) == 0`, then walks the chain from FirstSlab (+0x2D/+0x2E) through each tile's next link (+0x04/+0x05), testing the tile's flag word (+0x08 & 0x1000) and its state bits (+0x0B & 0x1E), calls 0x00452B70 and, for the final state, Player_SellRoomTile (0x004C5DB0, a direct callee), which reaches the room rebuild (Room_RemoveTile 0x004ED440, Room_Rebuild 0x004EDFC0) and its draws on the world stream. Read in outline from the decompile and a call scan; the tile states and what 0x00452B70 does are unread. Ours: BridgeBurnSystem is a per-tile countdown (WoodBridgeLifeOnLavaSeconds, Q67), run in the work phase, not a room-walk step.
