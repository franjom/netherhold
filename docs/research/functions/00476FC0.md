It indexes the state table as `0x006DA8B0 + 25 * stateId` with the handler at `+0`, which is how the table's frame was pinned (creature-state-table.md §2). Outside category 2 it runs the current category's update hook (`0x0069969C + cat * 0x40`); inside a walk it runs the hook only when the resume slot `+0xDF` and the saved caller `+0x40` share a category, and a hook returning 0 sets state 1 and ends the tick (state-categories.md §5.1, CONFIRMED). After the suspend countdown `+0x44` and the hook, a non-worker runs the need-anger routine `0x00478D00` and the needs routine `0x00477C20`, then the unhappy or angry chooser; the happy chooser belongs to idle state 89 instead (creature-needs-routine.md §1, idle-fallback.md §1). Built as `CreatureStateMachine.UpdatingCategory` (ADR-0185) and the needs order in `NeedRules` (ADR-0198).

The mood prelude is limited to nonworker keeper creatures after needs took
nothing. It excludes dying, state flag bit1, possessed-following +0x482,
Call to Arms participation, a prison owner, RebelActive, Horny, body +0x3E6
bits8/9, resolved combat, and state group7; content mood skips it. Angry
failure falls directly into unhappy, and only two failures permit the
current handler. The Fleeing raw-idle engine path maps its existing role,
need, captivity, combat and participation guards; body bits8/9 and +0x482
have no modeled counterparts. Its transient marker suppresses later duplicate
selection but does not suppress anger arithmetic, and resets before each turn.
This mapping is STRONGLY INFERRED; the full legacy dispatcher remains partial.

The prelude's +0x3E6 bit-3 test (0x004771A1/0x004771A7) is RebelActive: a rebel skips both mood choosers and runs its current handler. [controller+0x3C] at 0x0047721E is creature +0x28A, the mood class (2 calls the angry chooser).
