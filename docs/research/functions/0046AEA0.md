Needs `0x00453E90(owner)` on the wall for reinforce, `0x00453E30` for repair, each a separate pass. Sides are tried from the one `0x004805F0` picks for the imp's tile against the wall, turning by its step; a side counts when world slot `+0x1FC` passes and the side tile is not solid and is the imp's. The measure is to the wall tile's centre, not to the reinforce point (`0x00698AE0`, the middle of the touching edge, which is where the walk goes), and a nearer held side is stolen before a later free side is tried. It walks first and kicks the holder with `0x0048AD30(10)` only after its walk started; a dead or reused holder id refuses the side. The task is raised only when a side is taken, and freed again (`0x00508040`) when the walk fails on a fresh task, the next side then tried (imp-task-stealing.md §2, §4). Ours is `TryReinforceAt`, called per ring tile by Tier B (ADR-0291); repair is not a job in ours.
