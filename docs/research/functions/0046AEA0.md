Needs `0x00453E90(owner)` on the wall for reinforce, `0x00453E30` for repair, each a separate pass. Sides are tried from the one `0x004805F0` picks for the imp's tile against the wall, turning by its step; a side counts when world slot `+0x1FC` passes and the side tile is not solid and is the imp's. The measure is to the wall tile's centre, not to the reinforce point (`0x00698AE0`, the middle of the touching edge, which is where the walk goes), and a nearer held side is stolen before a later free side is tried. It walks first and kicks the holder with `0x0048AD30(10)` only after its walk started; a dead or reused holder id refuses the side; a refused kick after the walk started returns 0 with the imp walking and jobless. A free side with no entry yet raises one (`0x00507BB0`); a failed raise returns 0 for the whole handler, so the repair pass never runs. When the walk to a free side fails, the entry is deleted (`0x00508040`) and the next side is tried with none, whether the entry was just raised or came from `0x00508200` (TaskPool_Find) with other sides possibly held; the repair pass (kind 16, resume `0x17`) does the same (imp-task-stealing.md §2, §4). Ours is `TryReinforceAt`, called per ring tile by Tier B (ADR-0291). The other imps on the wall lose the task: state 22's handler (at `0x00466390`, no function defined in Ghidra) checks `0x00508070(+0x8A, 6)` at `0x004663C4` whenever `0x00496D00` reports no action playing (no action id, or 0x15), and on a miss clears `+0x8A` and sets 10 (read 2026-10-06). Built as ADR-0380 (`ForgetReinforceTask`, with the not-solid side test ours lacked): ours drops them at once, where DK2 drops a reinforcer at its next check and, INFERRED (the walk states were not read), a walker on arrival. The not-solid test is `+0x17B` bit 0 at `0x0046AFD2`; `+0x1FC` (`0x00509090`) is only a bounds check. Repair is a job in ours since ADR-0387.
