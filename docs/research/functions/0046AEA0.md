Needs `0x00453E90(owner)` on the wall for reinforce, `0x00453E30` for repair, each a separate pass. The kind-6 pass (`TaskPool_Find(6)`) loops the four sides; the kind-16 pass (`TaskPool_Find(0x10)`, predicate at `0x0046B1FB`) runs after it whenever no kind-6 side started a walk, whether `0x00453E90` failed or every side was held, unreachable or not the imp's. The two kinds are separate pool entries with their own side slots, so a kind-6 holder does not block a repairer. Sides are tried from the one `0x004805F0` picks for the imp's tile against the wall, turning by its step; a side counts when world slot `+0x1FC` passes and the side tile is not solid and is the imp's. The measure is to the wall tile's centre, not to the reinforce point (`0x00698AE0`, the middle of the touching edge, which is where the walk goes), and a nearer held side is stolen before a later free side is tried. It walks first and kicks the holder with `0x0048AD30(10)` only after its walk started; a dead or reused holder id refuses the side; a refused kick after the walk started returns 0 with the imp walking and jobless. A free side with no entry yet raises one (`0x00507BB0`); a failed raise returns 0 for the whole handler, so the repair pass never runs. When the walk to a free side fails, the entry is deleted (`0x00508040`) and the next side is tried with none, whether the entry was just raised or came from `0x00508200` (TaskPool_Find) with other sides possibly held; the repair pass (kind 16, resume `0x17`) does the same (imp-task-stealing.md §2, §4). Ours is `TryReinforceAt`, called per ring tile by Tier B (ADR-0291), walking the sides twice (kind 6, then kind 16) with the kind-6 face slots counted from routes (`ImpCrowding.SharesSlots`, ADR-0611) and the kind-16 slots read from the wall's persistent entry (`WorldState.RepairTaskEntries`, q811). The other imps on the wall lose the task: state 22's handler (at `0x00466390`, no function defined in Ghidra) checks `0x00508070(+0x8A, 6)` at `0x004663C4` whenever `0x00496D00` reports no action playing (no action id, or 0x15), and on a miss clears `+0x8A` and sets 10 (read 2026-10-06). Built as ADR-0380 (`ForgetReinforceTask`, now per kind): ours drops them at once, where DK2 drops a reinforcer at its next check and, INFERRED (the walk states were not read), a walker on arrival. The kind-16 pass, read in full 2026-11-15 (q811, STRONGLY INFERRED): `0x00453E30(owner)` on the wall (`0x0046B1FB`, false returns 0); `TaskPool_Find(0x10)` at `0x0046B237` leaves the entry's id (0 when none) and, when found, `0x005081C0` copies the entry, whose `+0x21` is the id the slot reads use. Per side (from `0x004805F0`): bounds, side tile not solid, side tile's owner (`+8 & 0xFFF`) the imp's. The slot is `0x00508110(id, side, 0)` (skipped when the id is 0, so a wall with no entry reads as free); a zero slot is free, a non-zero one is a holder. **Free:** raise the entry when there is none (`0x0046B370`; a refused raise returns 0 for the whole handler), run the imp's own leave hook `0x004647D0`, walk (`0x004651A0`, state 0x17); a failed walk deletes the entry by id (`0x0046B3B6`) and tries the next side; a started walk stores `+0x8A` id, `+0x8C/+0x90` wall, `+0x94` side, `+0x96` 0, writes `Worker[side*3]` = the imp's thing id (`0x0046B53F`) and `+0x98` = 0x10. **Held** (`0x0046B3C3`): a dead holder (liveness byte `0x006ED540[id]`), a non-creature, one with `+0x252` zero, in state 0x33 or 0x34, or one that may not enter 10 (`0x00477310`) refuses the side (next side, entry kept); otherwise the asker must be strictly nearer the wall's centre (`(x<<12)+0x800`) than the holder's own `+0x1A/+0x1E`, else the next side; nothing excepts the asker itself, which is never nearer than itself. A nearer asker runs its leave hook and starts its walk first; a failed walk goes to the next side; a started one kicks the holder with `0x0048AD30(10)` (`0x0046B57A`, a refusal returns 0) and overwrites the slot (`0x0046B5C5`). The holder need not be repairing this wall: nothing checks its state or job, and nothing releases the slot when a repairer leaves 23 or 24 or finishes (see [0x004647D0](004647D0.md), [0x00466710](00466710.md)), so the holder is whoever last took the side. Ours: `WorldState.RepairTaskEntries` holds the four side holders per keeper and wall, `TryReinforceAt`'s kind-16 pass reads and writes them (`MayTakeRepairSide`), the dig tag removes the entry (`TileTagSystem`), an unreachable free side deletes it; the kind-6 pass still derives its holders from the workers' routes. Level 5 seed 1: imp 440 fled at tick 1694 holding the north side of wall (15,33), and imp 454's pass at 2106 found it held and not nearer, so it took the east side (16.17, 33.5).

The not-solid test is `+0x17B` bit 0 at `0x0046AFD2`; `+0x1FC` (`0x00509090`) is only a bounds check. Kind-6 selection uses `ReinforcementRules.CanStart` (ADR-0607); see [Q696/Q564](../wall-reinforce-eligibility.md).
