Needs `0x00453E90(owner)` on the wall for reinforce, `0x00453E30` for repair. Sides are tried from the facing one; a side counts when world slot `+0x1FC` passes and the side tile is not solid and is the imp's. The measure is to the wall tile's centre, not to the reinforce point (`0x00698AE0`, the middle of the touching edge), and a nearer held side is stolen before a later free side is tried. It walks first and kicks the holder with `0x0048AD30(10)` only after its walk started; a dead or reused holder id refuses the side. The task is raised only when a side is taken, and freed again (`0x00508040`) when the walk fails on a fresh task (imp-task-stealing.md §2, §4).
