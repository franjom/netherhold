Two instructions, read 2026-11-11: `FLD float ptr [0x00673F34]; RET 4`. The float at 0x00673F34 is 0x3F000000 = 0.5. Its one caller is SoundInstance_BindVoice 0x0060CDB0 at 0x0060CF4F, with the maximum distance as the argument, which it does not read. The result becomes the third float of the block that 0x00617510 hands to backend slot +0x70: the third member of the record QMixer_SetDistanceMapping 0x0061AE30 passes to QSWaveMixSetDistanceMapping, or, on the first-tried backend (A3D, HYPOTHESIS; 0x00629A10), the one value sent in a separate call after the min/max call. In QMIXER.DLL the third member is the `scale` of the distance mapping, a rolloff multiplier on the distance past the minimum: factor 1 within the minimum, `min / (min + scale * (d - min))` out to the maximum, 0 beyond it, so 0.5 gives `2 / (tiles + 1)` (STRONGLY INFERRED, [qmixer-distance.md](../qmixer-distance.md) section 1). On A3D what the separate call does with it was not established.
