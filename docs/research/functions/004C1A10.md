Three instructions. Its one rel32 call (CallScanAll) is `0x0050BA84` in `Level_InitTriggersAndStart`'s per-player loop, with `MaximumManaThreshold` (`0x006F29CC`) minus the current `+0x10EA`. That sets 200000 for each player whatever the number of hearts (Q76). The other writers of `+0x10EA` zero it (`0x004B8894`, `0x004B9271`).
