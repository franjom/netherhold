Runs when the turn is at least the last payment `player+0x9A` plus ticks per second, reached from the player tick for keepers only. After the payment, a standing Call to Arms whose upkeep exceeds the mana left sends its flag to state 20 and returns before the warning block; otherwise the low-mana threshold (variable 125, strictly below 100), the warning and scan interval (variable 110) and the imp removal floor (variable 94) follow. It is also a reader of variable 227 (upgraded Call to Arms upkeep, 70 %) and variable 116 `HypnotismManaDrainPerSecond`, shared with `0x004C17A0`. Both docs mark their rows STRONGLY INFERRED. ManaSystem and CallToArmsRules.AfterPayment mirror it.
