Runs when the turn is at least the last payment `player+0x9A` plus ticks per second, reached from the player tick for keepers only. After the payment, a standing Call to Arms whose upkeep exceeds the mana left sends its flag to state 20 and returns before the warning block; otherwise the low-mana threshold (variable 125, strictly below 100), the warning and scan interval (variable 110) and the imp removal floor (variable 94) follow. It is also a reader of variable 227 (upgraded Call to Arms upkeep, 70 %) and variable 116 `HypnotismManaDrainPerSecond`, shared with `0x004C17A0`. Both docs mark their rows STRONGLY INFERRED. ManaSystem and CallToArmsRules.AfterPayment mirror it. Read 2026-10-04 (Q39, QNEW-mana): mana `+0x82` moves in one step per payment, and the re-stamp of `+0x9A` is at `0x004C16B1` (the two exits `0x004C13F5` and `0x004C1550` skip it). A non-negative net clamps mana to the ceiling `+0x10EA` (`0x004C11C1`–`0x004C11E7`); a negative net floors it at 0. Nothing here drains excess at a rate.
