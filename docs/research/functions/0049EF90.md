Called on an object. While its height (+0x26) is below +0x22 it only adjusts flags and returns. On landing it reads a creature id from +0x142 and clears it if that thing is gone or is not a creature. The landing arms:
- A room-held object (def +0xF4 bit 0x40) on a room other than type 13 or 5 is handled first.
- With no creature, a Gold, Gold Bag or Gold Pile merges into another gold object in its own third-of-a-tile cell (0x555 bands) and is destroyed, or is kept and handed to the neutral owner (0x006F2548).
- With a creature and gold type 1 to 3, the creature is paid:
  1. half of the object's GoldHeld (+0x14A) is credited through Purse_Receive 0x0048AE70;
  2. 0x0048E810 gets the negated owed wage (+0x470), and +0x3EA bit 5 is set;
  3. pay anger (bucket 4) is forgotten when nothing is owed;
  4. anger bucket 5 is lowered by definition +0x6E1;
  5. the object is destroyed.
- With a creature and type 9 (Chicken), the creature feeds (0x00463A10) and the object is destroyed.

It reads as the keeper dropping gold or a chicken on a creature. What writes +0x142 (presumably the Hand drop) was not read, nor were the other arms (types 9 and 10 without a creature). It is not the walk-over pick-up, which lives in 0x00488930.
