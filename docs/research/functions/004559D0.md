When the new tag differs from the old: a non-zero old owner loses the terrain def's +0x187 from +0x10E2 and +0x189 from +0x10DE (the terrain being the tile's CURRENT terrain byte at tile+10, looked up in the table at 0x006DA4A8), and 0x004BE070(-1) runs; the tag is written; the new owner gains both. In game mode 4 with the restore bit (world +0x7239 & 0x80, distinct from the map-load bit 0x40) clear, a player numbered 3 - old or new owner - gets a vtable +0x344 call with the variable at 0x006F29D9. When the tag is unchanged and bit 0x80 is set (a restore), the owner's words gain the terrain's values once more. Called from Tile_SetTerrain (0x0044FDD9), Room_TransferOwnership (0x004E5794), 0x0044F370 and 0x00450770. So the rate word is a running sum of each owned tile's gain AS ITS TERRAIN STOOD WHEN THE TILE CHANGED HANDS, not a sum over current terrains: a room tile taken by a rival moves 0, and the old owner keeps paying for it. Ours: TileState.KeptManaGain, cleared whenever a tile changes hands, with the terrain's ManaGain read live (ADR-0334); the old owner's leftover is not mirrored (Q521).

Whole tile owner setter has no visual-effect request; capture and claim effects belong to its callers. See room-construction-effects.md section6.
