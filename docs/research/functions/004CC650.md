Arguments (x0, y0, x1, y1). Bumps the generation byte 0x006A11E8 (clearing the stamp array 0x006ECA14 when it wraps to 0) and empties the stack 0x006E6F48. Seeds by 0x004CC3D0 from (x0, y0), (x0, y1), (x1, y0), (x1, y1) in that order. Then pops the top triangle and tries its edges 0, 1, 2 in order against 0x004CC190; on a hit it increments its counter, calls the flip 0x004C9FC0 and ignores the result (a flip the constraint bits refuse still counts), returns the counter at once when the stack depth plus 4 exceeds 999 (so at about 996 entries), and otherwise pushes the triangle's neighbours in slots 0, 1, 2 that are live (+0xC != 0xFF), unstamped and not solid (bit 3), stamping them; then it goes on to the next edge of the same triangle, rewritten if the flip happened. Returns the counter when the stack empties. A popped triangle is never re-pushed by its own hits, since it is stamped. Called after each inserted rectangle and once over the whole rebuild (0x004CE770). Ours sweeps touched slots instead (NavMesh.Coarsen), argued equal for a whole-map build only.
