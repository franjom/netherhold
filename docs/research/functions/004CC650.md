Arguments (x0, y0, x1, y1). Bumps the generation byte 0x006A11E8 (clearing the stamp array 0x006ECA14 when it wraps to 0) and empties the stack 0x006E6F48. Seeds from the four corners by 0x004CC3D0 (unread). Pops a triangle and tries edges 0..2 against 0x004CC190. After each accepted flip (0x004C9FC0) it returns the flip count at once when the stack depth plus 4 exceeds 999, so at about 996 entries, keeping the flip just made. Otherwise it pushes the flipped triangle's neighbours that are live (+0xC != 0xFF), unstamped and not solid (bit 3), stamping them. Returns the flip count when the stack empties. Called after each inserted rectangle and once over the whole rebuild (0x004CE770). Ours sweeps touched slots instead (NavMesh.Coarsen), argued equal for a whole-map build only.
