Its one caller is `Creature_DeathBookkeeping` (`0x0048833E`). So the victory nibble for a creature's attackers is given **at its death**, when its health is at or below 0. A creature that collapses while in the Combat category has already released its attackers through the leave hook, with no nibble (combat-category-hooks.md). Ours gives the nibble at the collapse (`VictoryRules.TargetDied`). That this disagrees with DK2 is inferred, not read: `spFighting`'s own dead-target test was not read (Q511).
