role: Walks a creature's sixteen attacker slots (melee +0x514 then spell +0x504 per index), calling 0x0045E4A0 on each attacker with the creature's id and clearing the slot.

Its one caller is `Creature_DeathBookkeeping` (`0x0048833E`). So the victory nibble for a creature's attackers is given **at its death**, when its health is at or below 0. A creature that collapses while in the Combat category has already released its attackers through the leave hook, with no nibble (combat-category-hooks.md). A victim collapsing outside the category gives none either: spFighting's dead-target test is slot liveness and its step 5 drops a dying (unattackable) target, unregistering the attacker, so when the victim dies sixty seconds later no attacker holds it (combat-category-hooks.md §5). Ours still gives the nibble at the collapse of a victim outside 22 (`VictoryRules.TargetDied`, Q511).
