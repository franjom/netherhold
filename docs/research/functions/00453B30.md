It reads the neighbours at minus and plus the row stride and minus and plus 16 bytes; one counts when the player owns it and its terrain is Ownable and not Solid, so a keeper's own walls do not start a claim (imp-task-priority.md §3, imp-claim-path.md §4). The claim handler, the claim states 15, 16, 18 and 22 and the kind-14 follow-up after a dig (with `0x00453FC0`) all require it (imp-state-exits.md, imp-claim-path.md §7), and the placement predicate 0x0044F630 asks it for water and lava (ECX the tile, the player tag pushed; bridge-placement.md §1). Read from the binary; built by ADR-0285 as `TouchesOwnLand` and TileWorkSystem.cs's kind-14 raise, which matched the Level 2 live capture's claim order, and since ADR-0304 as `OwnFloorRules.Touches`, shared by claims and room placement. The confirmed wall-support reading is in wall-support-after-claim.md section 1.
