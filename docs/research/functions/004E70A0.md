One of the eleven shipped dumpers (debug-dumpers.md lists it by its body address 0x004E70B6, 122 fields, with room-object.md as the layout home). It prints the three occupancy list heads (CreatureList, ObjectList, DeadBodyList), TotalCapacity and TotalFighterCapacity, the four Used*Capacity counters, Functional, Size, Destroyed and Health, then switches on room type; for the Prison (0x0B) it adds SafeCreatureCapacity, PrisonJailBroken, DoorTileX/Y, DoorOpen, DelayAutoCloseDoorCount and the drip and flies effect counters. Its printed names are how the room record's offsets were named. Read statically; unlike the world log it has not been run from Cheat Engine in the docs, hence LIKELY. Nothing in src/ mirrors it.
