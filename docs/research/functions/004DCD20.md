A thiscall on the room (no stack arguments). It sits in the third field of the byte-identical handler records of room types 3 and 25, slot +0x09 of the table at 0x006A1E9F, so it runs after the rebuilds that change a room's tiles. It first calls `0x004E6BC0`, a room pass that the rebuilds and the level start also call directly. Then, only when the slab count `room+0xA7` is 9, it walks the slab chain from `room+0x2D`/`+0x2E` (next slab at tile `+4`); at the fifth slab it calls `0x00454FD0(x, y, 4)`, the room-side wrapper of the notifying navigation-byte setter `0x004D1930`, and creates a light through the same world slot +0xA4 the wall torches use, at the tile centre (x*0x1000+0x800, y*0x1000+0x800, height 2000) with the literals 0xFF, 0x21, 0x1A, 0x80, 0x2800 and 0x15, clearing bit 0 of the created light's `+0x72`. In a map-loaded 3x3 portal the fifth slab is the centre, which is the byte-4 portal hole captured on Level 7 (`NavBytes.PortalHole`). The navigation byte is built in StructureBlocks; the presentation source remains unbuilt pending refresh multiplicity.
