A keeper-side handler, separate from the creature predicate `0x0048E6A0`: it tests hand capacity and repeated ids, branches on thing class, sets in-hand and unlinks the held thing through `0x004B4F60` while owner-list links stay (owned counts include held creatures, Q268). For a Combat Pit fighter it clears the fighter mark and forced target (`0x0048F3F0`); it does not touch the drop earmark. The keeper AI calls it with a tag (retreat, reinforcement). STRONGLY INFERRED from the binary in creature-pickup.md and combat-pit.md. Mirrored by HandSystem and HandRules, with CombatPitRules.Unmark for the pit branch.

Insert, not prepare (read 2026-11-10, disassembly, STRONGLY INFERRED). It returns 0 and does nothing when the creature is already in the hand, the id is not live, the hand is full (count +0x1BF at or above the maximum +0x1C0), vtable +0x28 is nonzero, the creature has +0x4A3 nonzero and is not the dropper's own (it calls 0x0048EE90 and returns 0), +0x3E6 bit 21 is set, the state is 51 or 52, or SetState(78) (0x0048AD30 with 0x4E) fails. On success it kicks to 78, sets the in-hand bit, stamps +0x4BF, calls 0x004B4F60, clears +0x3E6 bit 15 (CombatPitFighter), calls 0x0048F3F0 (clears the forced target), zeroes the words +0x64, +0x68 and +0x66, adds the id to the hand list and increments the count.
