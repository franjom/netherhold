A keeper-side handler, separate from the creature predicate `0x0048E6A0`: it tests hand capacity and repeated ids, branches on thing class, sets in-hand and unlinks the held thing through `0x004B4F60` while owner-list links stay (owned counts include held creatures, Q268). For a Combat Pit fighter it clears the fighter mark and forced target (`0x0048F3F0`); it does not touch the drop earmark. The keeper AI calls it with a tag (retreat, reinforcement).
