address: 0x004CB170
name: NavMesh_RemoveDegree4Vertex
role: Removes a vertex of degree 4 by re-joining its four triangles into two across one diagonal, freeing two triangle slots and the point slot.
confidence: LIKELY
status: READ
evidence: docs/research/navigation-partial-rebuild.md#1-what-the-binary-does

Arguments (triangle, vertex slot). Walks the four-triangle fan (given, second, third, fourth) and returns 0 if the fourth's next neighbour is not the given triangle. It does not guard against an open fan (-1 neighbours); its caller 0x004CAF40 reports a hull point as -1 first.

Two 64-bit orientation tests choose the diagonal. When the first is negative and the second positive, it frees the given triangle and the third and keeps the second and fourth. Otherwise (the fall-through) it frees the second and fourth and keeps the given triangle and the third. The kept pair's corners and neighbour links are rewritten, and the region ids of the two freed triangles are cleared. Both freed triangles go onto the triangle free list at 0x006A11D4 (+0xC = 0xFF, live count 0x006ECA30 decremented), which the allocator 0x004CA820 pops first. Both kept triangles get width classes recomputed (0x004C8F50), and the point goes onto the list at 0x006A11D8 (+2 = 0x8000, count 0x006ECA38 decremented). Returns 1.
