Read through `0x004D9A1A`. `0x004D96A4` tests byte `+0x9A` bit 0 and returns when set (meaning unread). Then `0x004F3A00`; then if GoldTally `+0x49` differs from 16 × `MaxGoldPerTreasuryTile` (`0x006F25A4`) and `turn & 0xF == 0`, the sweep `0x004F47E0` (`0x004D96B6`–`0x004D96D1`), which itself runs for a Heart only while room `+0x51` (SuckGold) is non-zero (`CMP AL,5`, then `TEST [ECX+0x51]`, `JZ 0x004F48D6`). Whether a Heart's GoldTally can ever equal the compare value is not reconciled: `0x004F4700` caps the Heart at 16 × `0x006F25A8`, while `00466AC0` records 48,000, which equals it. Called from `0x004D9A60`, the type-5 record's slot `+0x15`.

Its responder dispatch (0x004D96F0-0x004D9A1A): when a creature of a player passing 0x004BF390, with +0x3EA bit 6 set, is within the radius at 0x006F2964 of the heart, the heart owner's creatures within the radius at 0x006F2973 are walked to state 254, refusing Horny, Call to Arms, combat, a failed 0x0045A6D0(0), health at or below a quarter, state group 3 only (0x004D9976), a dropped job +0x2BC and one already heading to 254. Unlike the alarm and trap dispatches it does not refuse the Angry mood class or group 7.
