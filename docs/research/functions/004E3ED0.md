It walks the room's auto-placed-object list, matches each object type against the definition's furniture ids, and stores the result at 0x004E4038 into the TotalCapacity field. Library, Training Room, Workshop and Casino get zero with no match and otherwise matching objects + 1; the Torture Chamber gets the plain count. The switch is on type - 3, bounded at 13, through the table at 0x004E42C0: entries 1, 2, 5 and 6 (Hatchery, Heart, Wooden Bridge, Guard Room) and every type outside 3-16 (Treasury, Lair, Stone Bridge, the hero rooms) go to 0x004E429A, which stores the word at +0xA7, the slab count; the Portal's entry stores 0, and the Prison's arm leaves 0xFFFF in the result while its own count goes to +0x4B. The owner confirmed the Training Room arm on 2026-09-25 (a 3x3 on open ground shows 2; wall Training Targets count too). The value feeds trigger condition type 68, room capacity tests (state-category-hooks.md) and the portal attraction contribution 0x004DCE70. Built as AutoFurnitureRules.TryTotalCapacity, used by RoomCapacityRules and AttractionBudget; the default arm since ADR-0440; the Portal, Prison and Graveyard arms are left unread there (the Graveyard's is FloorSlotCount, the Prison's PrisonRules.Capacity); condition 68 is not yet wired.
