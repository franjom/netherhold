It is one of only three writers of the level byte +0x403 (store at 0x0048B06A), with the constructor's 1 and the increment 0x0048B120 (verification-2026-09-26.md section 2). It has three callers: vampire reincarnation 0x0048FFC0 (combat.md section 6.3), and two Hero Lair sites, 0x0049C2A0 and one at 0x005143C6, that walk a creature's level toward a target byte and call this while the level is too high (creature-levels.md section 2.1). So it is a step in setting a level, not a penalty, except in reincarnation. combat.md sections 4 and 6.3 now record the three callers. Not built: CreatureLevelRules only raises levels.

Suspected DK2 defect: DK2-023 (issue #2), independently reviewed 2026-11-13, disposition defer (ADR-0783). After DEC at 0x0048B06A, INC at 0x0048B070 restores the old level for the second maximum, so the rescale keeps health except for 12.12 truncation (about -1), unlike level-up 0x0048B120; a reincarnated Vampire (0x004900E7) keeps the old level's health; see creature-death.md.
