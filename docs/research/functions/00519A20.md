A second recursive walk that resolves ids like the evaluator 0x00518150 and applies a flag to each record; it is the only caller of the two setters, 0x00508660 for conditions (bit 0 of +0x10, plus repeat byte +0x0F set to 1 on enable, 0 on disable) and 0x00508840 for actions (bit 0 only). Its condition arm follows +0x0C (child) then +0x0A (next); its action arm follows only +0x0A, so anything hung under an action is never re-armed. Callers: the level initialiser 0x0050B5B0 disables the 24 party subtrees at load, and the action 14 handler 0x00519F90 enables a party's subtree at runtime, which makes re-armed actions one-shot because the repeat byte becomes 1 (232 shipped conditions carry 255). trigger-tree.md rates this strongly inferred from instructions, hence LIKELY.
