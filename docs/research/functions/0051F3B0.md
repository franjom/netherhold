Its value to research is structural: it moves the melee block def+0x5C0 to def+0x5CE as dword, word, dword, dword, dword in order with no gap, which is what settled those field widths (MeleeRange, MeleeDamage, MeleeRecharge, the attack-gate interval, and an unnamed dword). It also appears as the third reference, beside the initialiser 0x00491DC0 and the loader 0x00492320, for fields such as the base-id bytes near def+0x770 and the zero-for-all creature fields, and every doc that meets it treats it as a load-then-store pair, never a consumer. combat-cadence.md lists the field boundaries as CONFIRMED (binary). Who calls it and when a definition is copied are not recorded. Our definitions are loaded once and never copied, so nothing mirrors it.
