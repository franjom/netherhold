A nonzero argument sets flag 0x40; zero clears it. The Good creature loader calls it at 0x005203E8 from THINGS Flags bit 6; the neutral and keeper loaders copy that byte under mask 0xF9. Level_InitTriggersAndStart reads the flag at 0x0050BDB1 and kicks the creature to 76, and its later local-player pass skips the sight update for flagged creatures in rooms 11, 12 or 16. ThingProjection copies the flag and enters 76; LevelPlacedCreatureRules resolves startup landing.
