It runs once a tick per creature: it is the only caller of the action runner 0x00496AA0 and calls the state dispatcher 0x00476FC0 (training.md section 6.2), and its two effect timers set to variable 19 times 10 are why the update rate is four a game second (ADR-0089). The order read in combat-acquisition.md section 1: fight countdowns, ProcessFight 0x0045E9F0 (skipped when possessed), 0x0045E4F0, the state handler, the attackability recompute 0x0045A8B0, then the mover. The balance-variable audit lists it as the reader of CannotLeaveModifyCreatureHealthPerSecond (variable 83), which ships as zero. It also holds the walk-over gold pick-up (gold-on-the-floor.md section 12, read 2026-09-30), which runs every tick with no early return before it. A keeper's unpossessed worker (0x0048904E to 0x00489241) takes the first neutral gold pile (type 1, 2 or 3) within 0x555 of itself while its purse is below MaxGoldHeld less variable 74. Every other creature (0x00489246 to 0x0048954B) takes every neutral gold pile on its tile. It skips a Treasury tile, and takes nothing at all on a Casino tile, while +0x3F2 is non-zero, or in state 76. A Heart tile is not skipped, but a Heart's own tile piles are owned by the keeper, so only loose neutral piles there are taken; the worker arm tests no room type. The take is up to what the level-scaled MaxGoldHeld purse (+0x3FB) lacks, written directly rather than halved through Purse_Receive. The same walk destroys a loose neutral Chicken (type 9) outside a Portal or Hatchery tile and starts action 26. Ours mirrors the update in pieces, not as one routine; the listed files each reproduce one branch; the gold pick-up's worker arm is ImpTaskSystem.PickUpGoldInPassing, run first in each worker's update (ADR-0293), and the creature arm is WalkOverGoldSystem, run after movement and before the state choosers, skipping corpses (ADR-0300); the chicken side arm is not built. In the creature arm the take min(pile, MaxGoldHeld(level) - purse) is compared unsigned (0x004893B1 JC), so a purse already over its maximum would take the whole pile, and the purse is then clamped to 0x0048AB60's maximum; the tile's thing list is followed through each thing's +0xC.
