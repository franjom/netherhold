It runs once a tick per creature: it is the only caller of the action runner 0x00496AA0 and calls the state dispatcher 0x00476FC0 (training.md section 6.2), and its two effect timers set to variable 19 times 10 are why the update rate is four a game second (ADR-0089). The order read in combat-acquisition.md section 1: fight countdowns, ProcessFight 0x0045E9F0 (skipped when possessed), 0x0045E4F0, the state handler, the attackability recompute 0x0045A8B0, then the mover. Known branches inside it: the experience drain 0x00485880 at +0xC54, the mana-removal countdown (imp-mana-removal.md), the room-entry check 0x004E8660 that leads to rescue state 134, the claim-on-the-way taker 0x00464170, the fear update and the ThreatThing fields +0x533/+0x535. The balance-variable audit lists it as the reader of CannotLeaveModifyCreatureHealthPerSecond (variable 83), which ships as zero. It also holds the walk-over gold pick-up (gold-on-the-floor.md section 12, read 2026-09-30), which runs every tick with no early return before it. A keeper's unpossessed worker (0x0048904E to 0x00489241) walks only its own tile's thing list and takes the first neutral gold pile (type 1, 2 or 3) strictly within 0x555 of itself on both axes, min(pile, MaxGoldHeld - purse), then stops, while its purse is below MaxGoldHeld less variable 74. Every other creature (0x00489246 to 0x0048954B) takes every neutral gold pile on its tile. It skips a Treasury tile, and takes nothing at all on a Casino tile, while +0x3F2 is non-zero, or in state 76. A Heart tile is not skipped, but a Heart's own tile piles are owned by the keeper, so only loose neutral piles there are taken; the worker arm tests no room type. The take is up to what the level-scaled MaxGoldHeld purse (+0x3FB) lacks, written directly rather than halved through Purse_Receive. The same walk destroys a loose neutral Chicken (type 9) outside a Portal or Hatchery tile and starts action 26. Ours mirrors the update in pieces, not as one routine; the listed files each reproduce one branch; the gold pick-up's worker arm is ImpTaskSystem.PickUpGoldInPassing, run first in each worker's update (ADR-0293), and the creature arm is WalkOverGoldSystem, run after movement and before the state choosers, skipping corpses (ADR-0300); the chicken side arm is not built. In the creature arm the take min(pile, MaxGoldHeld(level) - purse) is compared unsigned (0x004893B1 JC), so a purse already over its maximum would take the whole pile, and the purse is then clamped to 0x0048AB60's maximum; the tile's thing list is followed through each thing's +0xC.

Call order within one creature's update (read 2026-09-30 over 0x00488930 to 0x0048A040): the gold block (worker arm 0x0048904E, creature arm 0x00489246 to 0x0048954B), then the experience drain 0x00485880 (0x00489584), then the action runner 0x00496AA0 (0x00489A68), then ProcessFight 0x0045E9F0 (0x00489AC7, skipped when possessed) and 0x0045E4F0 (0x00489ACE), then the state dispatcher 0x00476FC0 on the state machine at +0x24E (0x0048A030, skipped when possessed), then the attackability recompute 0x0045A8B0 (0x0048A037). No RET and no jump that skips the gold block lies before 0x0048A030; the one forward jump past the state call is the possessed test at 0x0048A028. So an imp that takes a pile in the worker arm is in state 10's handler with those hands in the same tick. Before its gold test the worker arm calls the claim-on-the-way taker 0x00464170 (0x00489076), only on a tick its tile changed and unless 0x00478050 returns 0x16. Ours (PickUpGoldInPassing) reads the imp's own tile in world order, after the claim on the way, which it tries from a worker job (effective category 3) with nothing carried (ADR-0378). Its gate is purse (+0x3FB) below MaxGoldHeld(level) minus the dword at 0x006F28B6, compared unsigned. The take is the smaller of the pile's gold (object +0x14A) and MaxGoldHeld(level) minus the purse. Variable 74 is 340 on every shipped level (0x0051CD90).
