RET 0xC. Uses the upgraded shot byte (+0x4B) and data (+0x4C/+0x50) when the row's Upgraded byte is set, unlike the creature path. Creates the shot at the position with heading 0 through 0x004B7CD0, writes +0x13D/+0x141 and calls 0x004AB830(pos); no launch, so the shot does not move. Debits mana, adds to +0x10F2 and stamps both clocks when not bypassed; spell 14 sets player flag 0x1000. Nine callers; the battle step's Inferno site is 0x00503672.
