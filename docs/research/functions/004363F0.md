A four-line helper taking `this` and one argument by pointer, returning an out-pointer that holds the product in 4096ths (combat-damage.md §1). It is the multiply in the work rate, the tabled delta times `+0x3AF + +0x3B3` (tile-work-efficiency.md), and in the damage formula. combat.md §5.1 once read its sibling helpers as clamps; combat-damage.md §1 corrected that, and combat.md §5.1 and combat-cadence.md §4 now point there. LIKELY. Our code does fixed-point arithmetic inline and has no mirror of the helper.
