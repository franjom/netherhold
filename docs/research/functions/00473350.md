A thiscall on the state object (+8 the creature, +0xC the world). When the turn is a multiple of ten, or the look-at latch +0xAC is set, it looks for a room object of type 0x31 to 0x33 in the prisoner's own room and turns toward it, latching +0xAC while the turn (0x004B2AE0) is not done. Otherwise, when 0x00496D00 reports no running action, it calls CreatureDef_HasArt(9) on the definition (no random draw), then random(100). With the art and a roll under 10 it draws random(3), adds 2 and starts action 41 (0x29) through 0x00496A30 at the creature's position; otherwise a roll under 20 sets state 115 (0x73). It always returns 1. Ours collapses 114 to 116 into PrisonerWanderSystem; the look-at turn is not modelled (ADR-0073, ADR-0301).
