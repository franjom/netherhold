A ruined room returns the whole amount. Each chain tile with no gold object gets one; the tile takes up to its capacity (0x006F25A4, or 0x006F25A8 for an unruined Heart), adds to room +0x49 and calls 0x004BA420 with flag 1. The remainder is returned. Read only for its return in 0x004BA420's spread loop.
