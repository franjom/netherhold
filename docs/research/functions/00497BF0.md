A fastcall on the imp, in the action table (0x006E4198 slot 10). It fetches the target tile (imp+0x38D), returns early when the tile gate 0x00454040 fails for the imp's owner, then picks a delta by the tile word +8 & 0xFFF against the neutral player 0x006F2548: equal, Unknown_17 (3) times the rate; otherwise ConvertRoomHealth (0x006F2574, -4000 divided by four at load) times the rate, the rate being imp+0x3AF plus +0x3B3. It applies the delta to the tile's room with 0x004E5300, passing the imp's owner, and announces a takeover when the tile's room id changed. It does not touch tile health. The tail sets bit 3 of imp+0xF1 and returns 1 only when bits 1 and 2 were clear. Our TileWork ConvertRoom is the one-tile case (Q500).
