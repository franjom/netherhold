Refuses a tile whose terrain lacks flag 0x80 (+0x17B) and whose owner is not the imp's keeper, and an imp for which `0x00464360(1)` says the hands are nearly full. Walks the tile's thing list (`+0x0C` links) for objects of class 2 owned by the neutral player with definition flag 0x40; for each it finds or raises the kind-7 task at the tile. A free slot: leave hook, walk to the pile's position (state 27 through 6), `+0x98` 7. Any non-zero holder, a dead one included, is first reach-tested (`0x00471630(pile, 0x80)`; unreachable: next pile); then a dead or non-creature holder is taken like a free slot, and a live one goes through the shared holder test and a strict nearer-the-pile compare, the holder kicked with `0x0048AD30(10)` before the walk; otherwise the next pile. Ours takes one tile's gold through `CanFetchFrom` (own tile or water) inside Tier B's ring scan (ADR-0291) and, since ADR-0343, inside the kind-7 ring scan of the same-kind retry (`TryTakeFloorGold`); the ring filter keeps water tiles out, so water gold is reached only from the unfiltered own tile; per-pile stealing is not built (Q155).
