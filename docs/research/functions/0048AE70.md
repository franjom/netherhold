The receiver is the creature and the argument an amount. It adds to +0x3FB, the field DK2's own dumper names GoldHeld, then caps at the definition's MaxGoldHeld (+0x69C). At levels 2 to 10 that cap is scaled by the stat row at 0x006E4154 + 0x13B, up to 120 percent (payday.md section 4.4). Callers:
- payday state 189 with half the wage taken;
- stealing, with half the take (steal-gold.md);
- the Casino's payout of variable 199 (state-category-hooks.md section 3.1);
- the hero party spawn, with a percentage of MaxGoldHeld while the creature is still level 1 (hero-parties-and-effect-generators.md);
- 0x00457560, which takes up to +0x3F4 from a Treasury or Heart tile and clears +0x3F2 when all is paid (a GUESS: the Casino big winner collecting its win);
- the possessed pick-up arm of 0x00514830, with half the take;
- the object landing 0x0049EF90, with half of gold dropped onto a creature;
- the mining strike 0x004971D0;
- three level-load creature spawns near 0x00520371.

The walk-over pick-up in 0x00488930 does not use it: it writes +0x3FB directly and keeps the whole take (gold-on-the-floor.md section 12). InitialGoldHeld is never read, so every purse starts empty. Built as PurseRules.Receive (ADR-0187).
