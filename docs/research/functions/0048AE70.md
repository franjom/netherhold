The receiver is the creature and the argument an amount. It adds to +0x3FB, the field DK2's own dumper names GoldHeld, then caps at the definition's MaxGoldHeld (+0x69C) scaled at levels 2 to 10 by the stat row at 0x006E4154 + 0x13B, up to 120 percent (payday.md section 4.4). Callers: payday state 189 with half the wage taken, stealing (steal-gold.md, half the take), the Casino's payout of variable 199 (state-category-hooks.md section 3.1), and the hero party spawn with a percentage of MaxGoldHeld while the creature is still level 1 (hero-parties-and-effect-generators.md). InitialGoldHeld is never read, so every purse starts empty. Built as PurseRules.Receive (ADR-0187).
