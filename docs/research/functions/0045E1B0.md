The Arch Pillar fallback ranks its candidates by unsigned dx_tiles squared + 2 * dy_tiles (0x0045E391-0x0045E3A3), a sum of a squared and a linear term, not a squared distance. Reviewed 2026-11-20 (0x0045E37F-0x0045E3AA): dx and dy are signed tile deltas, dx is squared (IMUL), dy is added twice (two ADDs, no IMUL), and the key is compared unsigned (JNC) against a best that starts at 0x7FFFFFFF; pass 1 (0x0045E281-0x0045E28C) squares both axes. So a pillar north of the creature with |dx| < sqrt(2|dy|) gives a negative sum, reads as huge and is never picked, and otherwise a pillar to the north ranks ahead of an equally near one to the south. No reading makes it intentional. Not built in src (state 238 is unbuilt); when built it must keep the key and the unsigned compare.
