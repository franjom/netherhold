Read whole (0x004CFC50-0x004CFD9C). cdecl (record*, cap); the record is A at +0, B at +8, the crossing at +0x10, the selector at +0x18. Octile length (longer axis plus half the shorter); under the cap it returns untouched. Selector 1 keeps B and moves A, anything else keeps A and moves B. The difference is shifted up 6, squared with the Q14 multiply (64-bit IMUL, SHRD 14) and summed; the divisor is Math_Isqrt (0x0065FAE0) of that sum, then shifted up 13, and 1 if that is zero. Each component is a Q14 divide that moves the numerator's sign into the divisor before IDIV, times (cap >> 8) - 1. Calls nothing else; no globals.
