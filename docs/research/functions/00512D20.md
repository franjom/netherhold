Three arms share one call to the pool adder 0x00507BB0 (kind, x, y) at 0x00512EBC. First test: tile byte +0x0B bit 0, the secret-door flag (set by the level loader and state 37 for a Secret Door through 0x00450FF0), files kind 11 without looking at the terrain. Otherwise the terrain must be Taggable (+0x17B bit 3), and it files kind 2 when the terrain's gold value word +0x185 is non-zero, kind 1 when zero. Every arm needs the pool's free count (+0x4650) above 20; at 20 or fewer it posts message 0 (secret door), 1 (gold) or 2 (rock) through 0x00517480 instead. Kinds 1 and 2 are CONFIRMED by an owner-placed live test (two rock and two gold marks arrived as two of each); kind 11 is the Secret Door task (Q288 answered from the binary, imp-task-objects-and-walks.md section 2), not yet seen in a capture. Ours models the marked set as a per-player tag bit in TileTagSystem, which is equivalent for area conditions 62 and 69 because both count kinds 1 and 2 together; it has no kind 11.
