Three arms share one call to the pool adder 0x00507BB0 (kind, x, y): kind 11 when tile flag byte +0x0B bit 0 is set, otherwise kind 2 or 1 on terrain flag +0x17B bit 3 (gold or not). It requires terrain flag 0x08 (our Taggable) and marks only while the pool's free-task count at +0x4650 is above 20. Kinds 1 and 2 are CONFIRMED by an owner-placed live test (two rock and two gold marks arrived as two of each); kind 11 has never been seen filled and stays unread (Q288). Ours models the marked set as a per-player tag bit in TileTagSystem, which is equivalent for area conditions 62 and 69 because both count kinds 1 and 2 together.
