Called on the definition with the level byte (+0x403). At level 1 it returns the word at +0x69C (MaxGoldHeld). Otherwise it multiplies that word by the signed word at [0x006E4154] + 0x13B + level * 2 and divides by 100. 0x006E4154 holds a pointer (0x006F2550), and 0x0051DC40 fills those cells from CreatureStats stat 6: 105, 110, 115 and 120 at levels 2 to 5, 120 up to level 10. An Imp's 1400 becomes 1470, 1540, 1610 and 1680, and nine captures show exactly those purses as the largest per level. The walk-over pick-up in 0x00488930 uses it to clamp the purse after each take. Several callers inline the same arithmetic instead of calling it: Purse_Receive, the steal take and the mining strike. The body is two lines and was read whole, hence CONFIRMED. CreatureLevelRules.MaxGoldHeld mirrors it. Two of ours bypass it and cap at the unscaled 1400: TileWorkSystem.HandTo and ImpTaskSystem.WouldOverfillItsHands.
