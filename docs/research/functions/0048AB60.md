Called on the definition with the level byte (+0x403). At level 1 it returns the word at +0x69C (MaxGoldHeld). Otherwise it multiplies that word by the signed word at 0x006E4154 + 0x13B + level * 2 and divides by 100. The walk-over pick-up in 0x00488930 uses it to clamp the purse after each take. Several callers inline the same arithmetic instead of calling it: Purse_Receive, the steal take and the mining strike. The body is two lines and was read whole, hence CONFIRMED.
