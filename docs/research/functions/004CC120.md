address: 0x004CC120
name: NavMesh_TriangleArea2
role: Twice a triangle's area: the absolute value of the cross product of two of its edges, in 64 bits.
confidence: LIKELY
status: READ
evidence: docs/research/navigation-partial-rebuild.md

Called by the rectangle stamp 0x004CC9B0 and its sweep 0x004CC850, which sum it over the triangles they stamp and stop when the sum reaches twice the rectangle's area, `(x1 - x0) * (y1 - y0) * 2`. Ours has no such exit: `NavMesh.InsertRectangle` stamps the triangles whose corners lie inside the rectangle and is done.
