Read 2026-10-01 (decompile and disassembly). The tile under the imp (game vtable `+0x1D4`) must carry a room (`+0x2` & 0xFFF) of the imp's owner; else it returns false (to state 1).

- The room's capacity: a live Dungeon Heart (type 5, `+0x9A` bit 0 clear) is `[0x006F25A4] << 4`, which is **16 × MaxGoldPerTreasuryTile (3000) = 48,000**, not the Heart tile cap `0x006F25A8` (1000) that the store chooser's capacity `0x004F4700` uses; a Treasury (type 1) is size (`+0xA7`) × `[0x006F25A4]`; any other room returns false.
- Space = capacity − stored (`room+0x49`), unsigned. Nonzero: amount = min(space, purse `+0x3FB`); `0x004F3C60(x, y, amount)` on the room, `Purse_Spend(amount)`, the deposit effect (`0xC4`).
- Then `Creature_SetState(10, 0)` whether or not anything went in.

So at a live Heart the hands always empty while it holds under 48,000, and what its 1000-per-tile tiles cannot take becomes a floor pile (0x004F3C60). Level 2's capture: imp 449 hands over 1,470 to a Heart at 15,995 at tick 1795 and fetches ~1,460 back off the floor at 1797.

Not built. Ours (`ImpTaskSystem.TryPutDown`) fills the tile it stands on and walks on with the rest. Built and measured 2026-10-01, then parked at the owner's call: faster deposits pushed more Level 1 seeds into our early-claimer case (replays 17 → 15 and 12 → 9 of 20), which is to be fixed first.
