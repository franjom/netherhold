Read 0x004488A0-0x00448A51 in the disassembly, for the draw-bit writers sweep of the possession-hide question (it has nothing to do with possession as far as read). The record's tile is its first dword (low word x, high word y); the player word at +8 is compared with the view's own player (vtable +0xBC, falling back to the world's local player at vtable +0x264) and a flag at +4 decides. The cell comes from view slot +0x1D8, and the thing from 0x00453670(cell, 4, &out). A record for the viewer's own player forces the flag to zero (0x004488FA). With the flag zero it sets bit 0 of thing +0xF3 and of the thing named by its word +0x101; otherwise it clears both, so the thing is shown to its own player and hidden from the other viewers. The thing's class and what the definition bit 0x20 means (a door or trap kind) are not tied to a name, and the sender of the record is UNREAD beyond one: the reveal arm of 0x004A55F0 posts a record of this layout (tile, 0, the door's owner) as command 0x11 at 0x004A65C4..0x004A6604. The table is the bridge's command table at 0x0066D0D8, (id, handler) pairs copied by 0x0043AAD0, with (0x11, 0x004488A0) at 0x0066D150; 0x0043B1E0 dispatches on the record's id at +0xC. Seven sites post command 0x11, each a flag and the marker write together: Imp state 37 (0x004675F2, flag 1, marker 1 at 0x00467604), Imp state 39 (0x00467869, flag 0, marker cleared at 0x0046781D), the reveal arm of 0x004A55F0 (0x004A6604, 0, 0), the door cleanup 0x004A5290 (0x004A543B, 0, 0), the door sale 0x004C3670 (0x004C37D4, 0, 0), the level-start door sweep of 0x0050B5B0 (0x0050BA02, 1, 1) and the door loader 0x00520F40 (0x005210F6, flag 1, with the marker left to the sweep). When no such thing stands on the tile the handler leaves at 0x00448A45 without writing the bit. The viewer it compares with (vtable +0xBC) is the word command 7 sets to the local player (0x00447D81), with the local player as the fallback while it is zero; no other writer was found.

The flag (EDX at 0x00448945) is also what 0x00443590 writes into bit 15 of the tile's presentation cell at 0x004489E7..0x004489EC, before the 3 by 3 redraw: the tile is drawn as wall for a viewer the door is hidden from (record 004434F0). The class-4 thing with definition bit 0x20 is a Secret Door (`DoorFlags.Secret`); the child at +0x101 is its lock object.
