Read 0x004488A0-0x00448A51 in the disassembly, for the draw-bit writers sweep of the possession-hide question (it has nothing to do with possession as far as read). The record's tile is its first dword (low word x, high word y); the player word at +8 is compared with the view's own player (vtable +0xBC, falling back to the world's local player at vtable +0x264) and a flag at +4 decides. The cell comes from view slot +0x1D8, and the thing from 0x00453670(cell, 4, &out). A record for the viewer's own player forces the flag to zero (0x004488FA). With the flag zero it sets bit 0 of thing +0xF3 and of the thing named by its word +0x101; otherwise it clears both, so the thing is shown to its own player and hidden from the other viewers. The thing's class and what the definition bit 0x20 means (a door or trap kind) are not tied to a name, and the sender of the record is UNREAD.
