It takes a message type, a player, a variant selector and an allocate flag, and has 86 callers (mentor-speech-and-notifications.md §3). Type rows come from the 100 x 10-byte table at 0x006A0B00 (low 13 flag bits are lifetime in seconds, 0x2000 NeedsTab, 0x4000 duplicates allowed, 0x8000 variant list). It refuses recipients with non-zero player status, honours world +0x72A1 bit 1 (except types 14/21/48/49), checks per-player suppression via 0x004C5530, returns an unexpired same-recipient same-type record unchanged when duplicates are off, and when the queue at world +0x2E54 is full evicts the oldest record of any player (computer-keeper-messages.md §1). Callers include combat (type 6), heart damage (type 1), rebellion (type 19), portal arrivals (0x0D/0x0E) and the unpaid-training message 0x37. PlayerMessageQueue.Post builds the admission and retention for types 1, 6, 52, 53 and 84; room, mood and subtype suppression are not built.
