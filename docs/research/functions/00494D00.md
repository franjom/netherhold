Called with the creature definition as `this` and the creature as its argument; it is the only reader of `def+0x5C4` (a word) apart from the definition copier `0x0051F3B0`. The multiplier is 12-bit fixed point: `+0x3E6` bit 7 gives (100 + global+0x31B)/100, `+0x3EA` bit 3 gives (200 + the same value)/200, `+0x3E6` bit 15 gives (100 + global+0x482, variable 225)/100, otherwise 1.0; it is then applied to the level-scaled base with a truncating `>> 12`. Its sole caller is the melee swing at 0x00498E30 (Q313, found both by call-site byte scan and by caller query). combat.md §5.1 first read the three flags as factors folded together through clamps; combat-damage.md §1-§2 corrected that to mutually exclusive `if / else if` arms over an add, a 12.12 divide and a 12.12 multiply (0x0043D750, 0x00441990, 0x004363F0), and that correction is the one built. combat-damage.md marks the arms and ceilings CONFIRMED from the binary; `MeleeDamageRules.StanceMultiplier`/`Swing` mirror it and `FightRules` calls it for every blow.
