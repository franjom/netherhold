The happy one of three near-identical choosers (unhappy `0x004782C0`, angry `0x004784C0`). It refuses in the combat category 22, diverts an `IsHorny` creature (`def+0x686` bit 14) to state 238, and passes only when `now - last >= 5 * variable 19` or `last == 0`, with `last` its own timestamp at `creature+0xFD` (job-selection.md §1). It then builds cumulative ranges from the happy job table and tries the rolled job via the dispatcher `0x00478800`. It is called from idle state 89's fallback, not from the per-turn dispatcher; on a failed pass it charges `AngerNoWork` through `0x00478B80(3, def+0x6C7, 3)` (idle-fallback.md §1, ADR-0228).
