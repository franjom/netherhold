The creature update 0x00488930 calls it with (room, creature) on a turn the creature moved to a new tile, for one on the ground, not dying and not attached to a room (+0x3B9 zero). The Prison and Torture Chamber arms need a non-worker, a room whose UsedCapacity +0x6B is non-zero, creature Flags +0x46F bit 5 (its only two reads in the binary, trigger-dispatch-tables.md), a room owner not allied to the creature (or neutral), and a category test (not 14 and not heading for 122 in a Prison; not 15 or 22 in a Torture Chamber); then, finding an allied non-dying captive on the room's attached list, it sets the stepping creature itself to 122 or 134 (MOV ECX,EDI at 0x004E879C and 0x004E8857). On a Library tile outside category 4 or a Training Room tile outside category 13 it calls the disturbed-room routine 0x004E88A0. The rescue arms are CONFIRMED and built (ADR-0201 for 134, ADR-0229 for 122) in RescueRules, JailBreakRules and RescueSystem; the disturbed-room arm is not built (idle-fallback.md §6, verification-2026-09-26.md).
