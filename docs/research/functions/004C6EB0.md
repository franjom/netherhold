Called as (player, roomId). The portal attraction gate 0x004DC810 uses it for its kind-0 room requirement, so the size a creature asks for is the keeper's total of that room type, not one room's: five 5-tile Hatcheries meet the Bile Demon's 25 (portals.md §3, marked CONFIRMED from the binary, refuting OpenKeeper's one-room reading). The player trigger condition type 24 also calls it when a room id is given, and does not truncate the sum to a byte (room-conditions.md). Built as the summation in PortalRules.IsAttracted.
