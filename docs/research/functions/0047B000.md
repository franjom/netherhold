At 0x0047B07D it masks the amount to a word and calls 0x004C2DC0 with it and the creature unconditionally, then returns 1 (0x0047B089): no zero test, so a creature with no research rate stays in its Library state. With a zero amount the adder adds no points (every bonus term is multiplied by the amount) but still runs its tail: the target re-test, 0x004C30F0, possibly Research_ChooseTarget, and the completion test 0x00507040.
