It indexes the 24-slot party table at world +0x4030 (stride 0x208, 16 member records of 0x20 from +0x08), arms the party's subtree through 0x00519A20 with flag 1, and creates each member through 0x00519B20, writing the creature Flags bits through the same setters action type 1 uses (at +0x5B4 and +0x5E7). A non-zero PartyType makes slot 0 a Dwarf and draws other slots from MpdRandomInvader types.
