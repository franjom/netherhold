Zero means "any" on each axis, and both zero short-circuits to the rectangle's area without reading a tile. With a terrain named, ownership is the tile owner alone; with none named, the tile must also carry terrain flag 0x04 (Ownable), an asymmetry of the original. Ownership masks tile +0x08 with 0xFFF and compares it to the u16 at player +0x04, not a player id directly (Q247, STRONGLY INFERRED to be the same). It computes an unsigned width with no bound check, so an inverted rectangle would read past the tile array; no shipped point has one and ours skips off-map tiles (Q248). Types 40 and 42 share an arm and are the same condition; 41 compares the count for equality with the rectangle's tile count.
