When speech is enabled (+4): zeroes the line count +0xC8; if a line plays (+0x150) it calls the sound object's slot +0x38 with callback 0x0066C77C (argument 0) or 0x0066F99C carrying the argument; then zeroes the subtitle count +0x14C and the last-subtitle time +0x15C. The shown subtitle +0x170/+0x174 is untouched. What the two callbacks do to the playing sample is unread, hence LIKELY. The skip passes 0x28.
