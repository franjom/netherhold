Fastcall on the room with an out position (three dwords). Two passes over the slab chain from +0x2D/+0x2E (next at tile +4): the first counts qualifying tiles, then 0x005558D0(count) on world +0x15A0 (0x004E84EE-0x004E8504: ADD EAX,0x15A0) picks one and the second pass writes its centre (x * 0x1000 + 0x800, z 0). With no qualifying tile it returns 0. Read by decompiler, offsets and loop checked in disassembly. 0x00454230 caches its wall-slot parity in the room (+0x92/+0x96) on first use; AutoFurnitureRules.WallSlots recomputes it from the chain each call, the same only while the first use ran in chain order. Ours: PrisonRules.RefusedBodyPoint for state 47; the wander and the jail break keep their own approximations.
