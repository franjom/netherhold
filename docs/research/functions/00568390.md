With the third argument non-zero and fewer than 16 subtitles (+0x14C): stores id - 1 at +0xCC + 8i and strlen(resolved text) * 0x60 ms beside it (the text through 0x00553A90 and 0x00553B90 into 0x0075BA60). Then, when speech is enabled (+4, +0x160) and the second argument is non-zero, refuses a duplicate (id, second) among the queued lines and appends (id, second, flag) to the 16 12-byte slots at +8 (count +0xC8).
