Arguments: out, vector, maximum. Length from the absolute components: under 1/16 tile the root of the exact squares; under 64 tiles the root of the components shifted down 8, shifted back up 8; above that the same with 18. A length under the maximum returns the vector unchanged; otherwise it scales by maximum/length. The middle band undershoots, so a velocity of 0.4615 passes a max_speed of 0.4600 (seen in seed01).
