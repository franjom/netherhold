Each pool entry has three holder slots per side; this reads one of them. A zero result means the handler takes the slot at once (claiming through 0x00508160, releasing through 0x00508040); a non-zero holder is taken only when the stealing rule allows it. It sits with the task table's find 0x00508200 and raise 0x00507BB0 and is used by the wall/seam walk, the claim, gold, reinforce and crate handlers. imp-task-stealing.md marks its findings CONFIRMED from the binary (no capture compared yet).
