It does nothing at level 10 or when the creature is finished; otherwise it stores the new level at +0x403 (0x0048B1E6), computes (health << 12) / oldMax, multiplies by the new maximum and shifts back, applies the difference through the health routine 0x004B2CE0, adjusts +0x3AF, plays effect 0x153 and sound 0x349, and hands a Combat Pit fighter that reaches the pit cap to 0x00476D30 (hero-parties-and-effect-generators.md, combat-pit.md). It has twenty call sites, eight in code Ghidra has no function for; the experience adder calls it at 0x004579D4, and the party spawn, triggers, the portal and the Hero Lair apply it repeatedly for multi-level grants. ADR-0234 said twelve callers; verification-2026-09-26.md section 2 corrected the count to twenty and confirmed the rescale.
