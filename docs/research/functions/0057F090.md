Item flag 0x2001 clears state bits 0x81 and sets 0x220. 0x2000 sets 0x4000 when global 0x00764BB8 bit 0x40 is set. Flag 2 clears 0xE0 and sets 0x201. 0x20 clears 0x400, and 4 clears 0x40. The highlight flag 0x200 is not among its inputs, so the overlay's blend comes from the overlay material itself.
