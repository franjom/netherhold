The variable-register OR at 0x004B9BBB uses mask 0x10000, not 0x80000. It therefore preserves the special-view bit; other notification behavior is outside Q611.

Timer speech and its gates (decompile re-read 2026-11-11, STRONGLY INFERRED). The whole function is behind two tests: it jumps to its epilogue (0x004B9EA2) unless world +0x72A1 bit 2 (mask 0x04, test at 0x004B974C, jump at 0x004B9753) is set (the bit world action 51 writes) and the player's +0x19A is 0 (0x004B9759, 0x004B9761). That covers the Horny sound, the 0x20000 flag and messages 0x5F, 0x5A, 0x59 and 0x58 as well as the countdown. Only the countdown block has two more gates: the game mode (0x0075AD4B) is not 4 and the LevelTimeLimit slot (world vtable +0x34C, the time left) is non-zero. Default: world initialization 0x005099A0 reads +0x72A1 at 0x00509CD9, ANDs it with 0xF7 and ORs in 0x07 and stores it (0x00509CE5), so bits 0 to 2 are on and bit 3 is clear until a level script changes bit 2 through action 51. The block compares the time left for equality with ticks-per-second (0x006F2590) times a number of seconds, one test per value, first match wins: 1800, 1200, 600, 300, 240, 180, 120 and 60 s enqueue speech ids 1 to 8; 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 s each clear the speech queue (argument 0) first and then enqueue ids 9 to 18. The speech enqueue, called with the id, the global at 0x0075BA3C and 0, has no local-player test. Equality means each id is said once, on the turn the counter passes the value.

Caller: Player_Tick 0x004B9EC0 at 0x004B9F19, the only caller. World_UpdateTurn 0x0050A450 reaches Player_Tick through World_TickPlayers 0x004C2310 (called at 0x0050A7F5) after the level time-limit countdown at 0x0050A64E, so HYPOTHESIS: the countdown has already counted down for the turn when this equality is tested.
