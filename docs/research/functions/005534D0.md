Read whole (decompile and disassembly). For each of 10, 14 and 0x12 it does the following:
- takes 0x00553690(size, width) and loads that type-4 resource with 0x0062DFF0 into 0x00741560, 0x00741564 and 0x00741568 in turn;
- calls 0x0062C470, 0x0062C440 and 0x0062C3C0 (the last with bytes FF FF FF FF 00) on the style objects 0x00741320 and 0x00741370, and a third.

The only caller is 0x00402D00, with the screen width. That 0x0062C470 attaches the font and 0x0062C3C0 sets the colour is INFERRED from the arguments; those two are unread, hence LIKELY. 0x0040A3E0 copies style 0x00741320 (0x0062C2D0), so the path subtitle is the base-10 font.
