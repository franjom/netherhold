With the new terrain def's id (+0x1C4) differing from the tile's terrain byte: if neither the old nor the new def has flag 0x10 (Room) in +0x17B, and the tile has an owner, and the world's LoadingLevel bit (+0x7239 & 0x40) is clear, the owner's +0x10E2 loses the old def's +0x187 (when the old id is non-zero) and gains the new def's; otherwise, when the new terrain id is 0x11 or 0x1A (the two bridges) the word of the player the tile is tagged to at that moment gains 1, and when the old one was a bridge that player's loses 1 - no owner test and no 0x40 test on this path, and since Tile_SetTerrain tags the tile AFTER this call, a bridge laid over water or lava (tagged neutral, non-ownable) credits the neutral player and a bridge removed debits its builder; a bridge replacing a bridge only adds. The bit is the map-load phase (0x40), not the restore (0x80). The terrain byte is then written. Consequence, matched by the Level 3 capture's rate word (+0x10E2 + upkeep = claimed path + reinforced wall + every room tile the keeper built, with the heart's 25 and the portal's 9 counting 0): a room laid over claimed floor keeps the floor's 1 on the books, a room sold back to floor adds nothing more, and a room the map laid never counted.
