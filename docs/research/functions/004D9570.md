fastcall (room). Repeats until none is left: finds a thing in the room's +0x0E list whose +300 byte is -0x71 and removes it (0x004E62D0, 0x004B7B10). Then zeroes room +0x55 and, while room +0x0A names an occupant, calls 0x0048AD30(1) and, if its +0x3B9 is still set, 0x004E5E00. Called by 0x004D8E60 and 0x004D8C70, whose roles are not read. Not built: ours has no eviction (Q504).
