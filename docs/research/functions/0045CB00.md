The name comes from the function's own assertion strings. It is 5,746 bytes and has twelve callers, six of them in spFighting (0x0045F030), which calls it whenever the target field is empty; a failed acquisition ends the fight. It runs six scans (including the friendly-spell scan F and the heart scan E), dispatches on FightStyle to the five selector arms (the NonFighter arm is 0x0045BDF0), writes +0x502 six times, and sets the +0x537/+0x539 target and countdown that steering reads. Its forced mode takes any thing id, which door blocking uses to aim at a door (door-blocking.md). combat-acquisition.md marks section 6's setup, scans and dispatch CONFIRMED (binary). Ours mirrors it for the four fighting arms in CombatTargetRules.
