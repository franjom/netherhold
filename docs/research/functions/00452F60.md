The applier every tile-work handler ends in: a negative delta subtracts from the health word at `tile+6` and floors at zero, a positive one adds and clamps at `0x7D00` (32000). At zero the tile becomes the terrain's destroyed type (`terrain+0x1C6`); at or above `MaxHealth` (`terrain+0x1AD`) it becomes the max-health type (`terrain+0x1C5`), through the terrain setter `0x0044FC40`. The health is the only accumulator, so an upward job costs the deficit (reinforcing Rock 495, claiming Dirt Path 659), which reversed ADR-0024 (ADR-0089). No overflow carry or chained destroy happens inside it (runtime-map.md). CONFIRMED by owner timings and run 15's per-application deltas (tile-work.md, ADR-0133); built in TileWork.cs. A tile that reaches zero while its room word (+2) is set is first unmade through Player_SellRoomTile (0x004C5DB0) on the tile's owner with (x, y, 0), a sale with no refund (0x004531B5), and the room's survivors keep its share of health (0x004ED440). No check stops it working a room tile: action 11 uses it on one.
