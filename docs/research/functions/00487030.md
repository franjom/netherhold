Every one of the sixty anger sites reaches it through the wrapper 0x00478B80 (bucket, amount, selector); its receiver is an int[8] at creature+0x26E holding six counters, the unhappy threshold at [6] and the mood band written back at [7]. It clamps only the changed counter to [0, 0xFFFD], then takes the maximum of the six (ties keep the lowest index): below [6] content, above 0x7FFF angry, otherwise unhappy. Nothing sums the counters. When world byte +0x72A1 bit 0 is clear the add is skipped but the mood still recomputed. Buckets 1 to 4 are food, lair, work and pay; 5 and 0 are this project's names (Q202). A percentage sibling at 0x004870E0 clamps the same way. Built in CreatureAngerRules (ADR-0081, the switch per ADR-0160).
