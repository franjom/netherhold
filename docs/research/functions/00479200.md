The names it prints pinned the state object's layout: +0x10 CurrentStateId, +0x18 StartedAt, +0x1C Age, +0x48 LastAteTime, +0x4C HungerNeed, +0x50 LastAttemptedHunger, +0x54 to +0x5C the sleep trio, +0x60 PayCheckedTime, +0x64 CurrentlyDoingJobId and +0xDF UtilityNewState, plus about thirty more including the ImpJobData block (eating.md 2). It does not print the object's +0x3C, which is why that field stays unnamed (state-categories.md 5). A diagnostic, not a mechanic: nothing in src/ mirrors it.
