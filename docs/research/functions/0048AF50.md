Called on the creature with an unsigned amount. If the amount is more than +0x3FB it sets the purse to zero; otherwise it subtracts the amount. It returns the new value. The body was read whole. Callers:
- the idle state 71 handler 0x00459180;
- the leaving arrival state 196 (0x0045A0D0), which empties the purse before removal;
- the imp's deposit state 29 (0x00466AC0);
- the Casino state 0x0047AD70, which also calls Purse_Receive;
- the steal states 0x00482EB0 and 169 (0x00482DC1 is inside 0x00482DB0);
- 0x004D6CB0.

PurseRules.Spend is the mirror.
