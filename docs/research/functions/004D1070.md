A thiscall with one stack argument (RET 4): `this` is the movement body embedded at `creature+0x124` (the stop routine `0x0048ECE0` passes `ECX = creature + 0x124` and the creature as the argument). It calls `0x004D0F60(creature, &creature+0x1A, 0)`, then `0x004D59A0(x, y, 0, 0)` with the creature's position, which stores position and velocity into the body at `+0x84`/`+0x88` and `+0x8C`/`+0x90`, each shifted up four. Nine callers, including the stop routine, the creature update (twice), the slap and the hand drop. It is the only place read here that zeroes the body's velocity, which is why whether 0x004D0F60 does so is left open.
