Third step of the per-tile chains 0x00469580 (Tier B) and 0x00469240 (drop scan), and the handler of the dispatcher's kind 8. Tile gate: the tile is the imp's keeper's, or its terrain has Water (+0x17B bit 7). It walks the tile's thing list in order; a candidate is an object (+0x12 == 2) of the neutral player ([0x006F2548]), not being dragged (vtable 0x0066D654 slot +0x74, 0x004B7250: bit 1 of +0x146), whose definition flags (+0xF4) carry 0x04, 0x10 or 0x08, tested in that order. 0x04 is skipped when the tile is in a room and the object's RoomId +0x138 is that room, else needs a Library (type 6) with space; 0x10 first calls the crate branch 0x00468910, before the shared latch is tested, and its success returns 1 at once, with no kind-8 reach test, find or raise, or holder steal; else it needs a Workshop (type 10); 0x08 needs a Library. The room search is 0x004C6610 on the keeper for the definition's byte +0x114; a failure is latched for the rest of the tile (one latch for 0x04, one shared by 0x10 and 0x08), and the room found is not kept. Then the object's position must pass 0x00471630(point, 0x80); the entry is found (0x00508200) or raised (0x00507BB0) per tile, and if neither the handler returns 0. Take: leave hook 0x004647D0, walk 0x004651A0(object position, 30, 0xE8, 0); success writes +0x8A, +0x8C/+0x90 = the entry's tile, +0x94 = +0x96 = 0, the slot and +0x98 = 8.
