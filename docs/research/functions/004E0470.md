Called on the player, it walks the keeper's rooms, keeps reachable lairs with a free slot (0x004E53B0(1): used + 1 at most the tile count), and measures each from the creature to the room's centre, keeping both the nearest overall and the nearest with no bed whose owner 0x0045C640 finds of the other alignment; the second wins when it exists (Q151). It tests nothing about size or whether the room is functional. With none it returns 0 and, at Lair availability 3, posts 41 with no Lair unless a build or sale is pending (0x004C7340/0x004C7360), else 43 for a Lair with a slot that 0x00471740 with option 2 (doors ignored) cannot reach, else 42 when a Lair the 0x80 route reached was full and more than 4 x ticks-per-second turns past its room+0x9F; Data1 is 2.
