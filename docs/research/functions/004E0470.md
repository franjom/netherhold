Called on the player, it walks the keeper's rooms, keeps reachable lairs with a free slot (0x004E53B0(1): used + 1 at most the tile count), and measures each from the creature to the room's centre, keeping both the nearest overall and the nearest with no bed whose owner 0x0045C640 finds of the other alignment; the second wins when it exists (Q151). It tests nothing about size or whether the room is functional. With none it returns 0 and, when the keeper may build lairs, posts no-lair, unreachable or full (41, 43, 42; full only after a grace period); computer-keeper-messages.md adds that a pending build or sale gates only the absent-room message.
