Called by 0x004CC9B0 when the four corner triangles alone do not cover the rectangle. Runs the slots from `DAT_006ECA2C - 1` down to 0, tests each live triangle's three vertices against the rectangle's bounding box, stamps the byte at +0xC and adds 0x004CC120's twice-area, returning once the sum equals `(x1 - x0) * (y1 - y0) * 2`. A test of every slot, by vertices, not a neighbour walk. Ours (`NavMesh.InsertRectangle`) stamps the same set, the triangles whose corners all lie inside the rectangle, by a flood from one of them; the set is the same since the rectangle's sides are constraints in both.
