Arguments (x, y, tx, ty, out triangle, out slot). Locates the vertex by 0x004C9B50 and returns 0 if the point is no vertex. Turns around the fan; at each step tests the next corner against the direction (tx - x, ty - y) with a 64-bit cross product. Returns the triangle and slot when the corner is collinear and in the same direction on both axes' signs. Returns 0 on reaching the hull or closing the fan without a match. Used by the side walks 0x004CE380 and 0x004CE410 and by 0x004CE0C0.
