Read in full 2026-10-02. thiscall on the room, (creature, out*), RET 8. Walks room +0xE through object +0x12A links for objects whose type byte (+0x12C) is 8 (wheel) or 0x3D (chair); for each, the device's tile thing list (+0xC links) is scanned for a class-0 thing other than the creature in state 0x83 or 0x88 (131 or 136), which skips the device. A free device on the creature's own cell is returned at once; otherwise the least squared distance (each axis difference squared >> 12). Returns 1 with *out, or 0.

The finder itself does not require the device to share the creature's cell; it prefers one that does and falls back to the nearest free one in the room. The same-cell requirement is enforced by its caller 0x00484110 (device cell equals the creature's cell, squared distance below 0x1000), which is why a strap never moves the victim to a device elsewhere (openkeeper-pr-audit-1000-1110.md, PR 1013). Other caller: the torture action handler 0x00499300.
