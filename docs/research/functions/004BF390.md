Receiver ECX is the player, the argument a player tag. Looks the tag up in the thing table, reads its compact number at +0x10FC and returns whether the byte at player+0x1AE+number is zero; the negation of 0x004BF3D0. Heroes and the neutral player are enemies unless a level's alliance record (0x0051DC90) says otherwise, which is why unowned land passes the tunnel test 0x004FDB90.
