Read whole with PyGhidra 12.1.4 on 2026-10-10. Rejects Neutral and Good owners,
missing targets and non-creature targets. Walks the owner's room list (player +0x36,
next +0x08) and skips every room whose type byte +0x18 is not 9, the Guard Room
(`0x00460BC8 CMP byte ptr [ECX+0x18],0x9`, `JNZ 0x00460C1E` to the next room). It then
tests the target's tile against that room's bounds (+0x49/+0x4B x, +0x4D/+0x4F y)
expanded by the low byte of variable 190 (GuardRoomReportingDistanceTiles), strictly
on all four sides. The flee handler uses the result to choose a strength count and
Scout alarm or withdrawal; the hero-reluctance arm of the acquirer uses it the same
way. NearOwnGuardRooms owns the test; its callers supply the owner and target tile.
