Read in full (disassembly, 2026-10-02). Thiscall on the creature, no stack arguments (plain `RET`), so it decides from the creature alone. In order:
1. A walk over a list from the owner (`+0x2A`, then `+0x10FD`) through the thing table `0x006EE540` that stores nothing: no effect.
2. `0x0048F3F0(me)`: clears `AttackingId` `+0x3F9` and `AttackAbnormalTargets` (`+0x3E6` bit 2), then jumps to `0x0045EF50`, which strikes me from my creature target's slots and zeroes my engagement `+0x502`. So this routine **does reach our `Unlink`**, after dropping the forced target.
3. An inline copy of that unlink (attack nibble 0, 1 or 3, then `0x0045AFC0` and `0x0045E450`). `+0x502` is already 0 after step 2, so it never runs.
4. Over the eight indices, the ranged slot `+0x514` then the melee `+0x504`: each attacker whose `+0x502` is me gets the victory nibble (`+0x529 |= 0xF`) **only if my health `+0x40` is at or below 0**, has `+0x502` cleared, and, with `AttackAbnormalTargets` set, `0x0048F3F0` (its forced target dropped). The slot is cleared whether or not the attacker still targeted me.
5. The same empty list walk.
6. Clears `+0x232` bit 7 and `+0x233` bit 0 (the hero bit the acquirer writes, combat-target-selection.md §3).

Callers (xrefs, 2026-10-02): the Combat leave hook `0x0047A980`, and both route helpers, `Travel_RouteToPoint` `0x00471890` and `Travel_RouteToRoom` (`0x00471B94`), when a walk takes a combat creature to a non-combat resume (travel-states.md §2). Built in `CombatRules.LeaveFight`.
