Its first test (0x004DC9AB-0x004DC9B8) shifts the owner's +0x76 right by 10 and masks 1. A clear bit returns 0 before anything else. The player dumper prints that bit as PortalsOpen (0x004BDCEA). portal-creature-action.md reads the rest: the room +0x2D/+0x2E centre in fixed point plus 0x1800, the factory 0x004B78D0 -> 0x004B7900, refusal on an unresolved owner or at 448 creatures, no search for a free tile, then body +0x3E6 bit 1, creature action 49 (0x31, Entrance), state 193 for a keeper Portal (278 for a Hero Portal), the room id at +0x4EB, and notifications 0x0D/0x0E. Exactly two callers (xrefs 2026-10-01): the pick 0x004DBF90 at 0x004DC5DA and the action-28 wrapper 0x004DC8F0 at 0x004DC944. Built as the PortalsOpen refusal in PortalSystem (ADR-0314) and as PortalRules.CreateArrival.

The +0x4EB stamp (2026-10-04) is a word write of the portal room's tag (room +0x04; EDI is the room, ECX on entry at 0x004DC9A4), on the keeper arm at 0x004DCC40 and the hero arm at 0x004DCC99. Creature_DeathBookkeeping reads it back at a death to decrement that room's tally +0x4D (Q508). The scans of +0x4EB writers found only these two and Creature_Construct's zero at 0x004878EC; that nothing else writes it, and so that a converted creature keeps the stamp, is STRONGLY INFERRED.
