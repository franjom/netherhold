Scans the player's nine-byte room rows (base `player+0x5A`, count `+0x56`; `TypeId` u8 at 0, `Availability` u32 at 1) downwards for the type and returns its availability, or 1 when absent. The idle fallback asks it for Training Room (7), Combat Pit (16) and Casino (15), and room conditions and the keeper AI use it too; 3 means available. Checked against the binary in the 2026-09-26 verification. WorldState.Availability mirrors it, including the default of 1.
