thiscall on the pool, (id, out). Indexes the pool by id & 0xFFFF at stride 36; requires byte +0x23 non-zero and word +0x21 equal to the id, then REP MOVSD 9 dwords into out (0x005081E5-0x005081EA). The kind-11 handler 0x0046CB80 calls it at 0x0046CC39 with out = EBP-0x64, which is how [EBP-0x43] holds the entry's id.
