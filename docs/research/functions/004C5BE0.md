Thiscall on the player, one argument, the room; returns 1. It calls 0x004EC900 with the room and a byte out-count, and when that succeeds adds the count (a negative number, one per room absorbed) to player +0x10F9. Called by action 10 (0x00497D8C) on the tile's new owner when the room's PlayerId changed. Ours needs no counterpart for the merge: rooms are derived from tiles, so neighbours of one terrain and owner are one room at once, and their summed deficit equals DK2's clamped sum (room-health.md section 2). The +0x10F9 tally is not mirrored.
