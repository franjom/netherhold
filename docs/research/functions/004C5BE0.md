Thiscall on the player, one argument, the room; returns 1. It calls 0x004EC900 with the room and a byte out-count, and when that succeeds adds the count (a negative number, one per room absorbed) to player +0x10F9. Called by action 10 (0x00497D8C) on the tile's new owner when the room's PlayerId changed. Ours derives the merged room from tiles and sums its deficit (room-health.md section 2). HatcheryRules.OnCaptureMerge preserves the captured room's generation stamp through the merge and clears the startup interval (ADR-0606). The +0x10F9 tally is not mirrored.
