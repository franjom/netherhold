Thiscall on the player with (type, units, creature position, out room, creature). For each room of the type in the player's room list (+0x36) with Room_HasCapacity(units), it measures the squared distance (each axis shifted down 12 before the sum) from the creature to the room's CentrePos (+0x27) and, when nearer than the best so far, asks Creature_CanRouteTo the room's floor-slot position (+0x2B/+0x2C) with options 0x80. Rooms whose occupants all pass Creature_ObjectsToAlignment are kept apart and preferred; otherwise the nearest routed room of either kind. Read by decompiler.
