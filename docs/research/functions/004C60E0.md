Thiscall on the player; walks its room list (+0x36, next +0x08). Distance is to the centre of the room's tile at +0x27 (x low word, y high word), squared per axis and shifted right 12, summed, unsigned, strict less-than, so the first room wins a tie. No distance cap, no visibility test. Used by Job_DestroyEnemyRooms 0x004582C0 and State179_GoToEnemyRoom 0x00458370 to pick the room to attack.
