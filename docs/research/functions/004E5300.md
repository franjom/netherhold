A thiscall on the room, two stack arguments: a pointer to the delta and a player id. A negative delta subtracts; a result under 1 calls Room_TransferOwnership with the player id, then sets health to the terrain's StartingHealth (terrain+0x1AB) times the tile count (room+0xA7). A non-negative delta adds and clamps at MaxHealth (terrain+0x1AD) times the tile count. The terrain comes from the room definition's terrain byte (def+0x44E) through slot 0x70 of the definition table. Callers and what they pass: the per-tick room update 0x004E4B00 (the definition's HealthGain, 0 in shipped data); action 10, 0x00497BF0 (the conversion rate); action 12 at 0x004981E3 (RepairRoomHealth, never started); Room_AddTile 0x004EC650 (StartingHealth); Room_Absorb 0x004ECB70 (the absorbed room's health); Level_InitTriggersAndStart at 0x0050B966 (Health / Size, for rooms of the player whose Number +0x10FC is 2, Neutral; it clamps away on shipped data, where StartingHealth equals MaxHealth); the map loader at 0x005215BA (minus Health x (100 - p) / 100 for a pre-placed room thing whose percentage p is not 100; the heart takes DungeonHeart_Build instead, and the two shipped non-100 room things are both hearts). The full-state captures confirm it: Level 1 and Level 2's neutral portal falls 90000 by 4000 or 4499 a tick and resets to 90000 under its new owner, and built rooms grow by 10000 a tile. RoomHealthRules mirrors it (Q500).

Whole room health applier emits no effect itself; completed conversion calls the ownership transfer that emits one effect8 per slab. See room-construction-effects.md section6.
