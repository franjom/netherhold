A thiscall on the room, two stack arguments: a pointer to the delta and a player id. A negative delta subtracts; a result under 1 calls Room_TransferOwnership with the player id, then sets health to the terrain's StartingHealth (terrain+0x1AB) times the tile count (room+0xA7). A non-negative delta adds and clamps at MaxHealth (terrain+0x1AD) times the tile count. The terrain comes from the room definition's terrain byte (def+0x44E) through slot 0x70 of the definition table. Callers: the per-tick room update 0x004E4B00 (the definition's HealthGain, 0 in shipped data), the imp conversion handler 0x00497BF0, the repair at 0x004981E3, tile added 0x004EC650, merge 0x004ECB70, a level-start pass 0x0050B966 and 0x005215BA. Nothing in src mirrors it (Q500).
