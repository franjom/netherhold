role: Makes the segment between two mesh points an edge of the mesh: from the target end it turns the fan toward the other end, recurses where a vertex lies on the segment, walks the triangles the segment crosses carrying an edge on one side, and runs the flip sweep 0x004CBBD0 where the next apex falls beyond that edge; then swaps the ends and repeats until the edge exists. Arguments (ax, ay, bx, by) in tiles; returns at once when equal. Locates the target (b) as a vertex through 0x004C9B50 (slot s of triangle T); each pass: checks the current end (a) is a vertex, turns the fan around the target toward a through 0x004C9540 (which returns e = next(s), the edge opposite the target), and returns if the vertex at next(s) is a; else U is the neighbour across edge s (the edge leaving the target), bU its back slot, N the vertex at bU of U (the far end of that edge): if `Orient(b, a, N) == 0` it recurses with (b, N) then (a, N) and returns. Otherwise it walks: V = the neighbour across e of the current triangle, bV its back slot, e = next(bV), apex = the vertex at prev(bV); if `Orient(b, a, apex) >= 0` it reads X = the neighbour across e of V, bX its back slot, P1 = the vertex at bX of X, and with V2 = the vertex at next(bU) of U and V1 = the vertex at bU of U tests `Orient(V2, V1, P1) < 0`: when true it calls 0x004CBBD0(U, bU, X) and leaves the walk; when false it sets e = prev(bV), U = X, bU = bX. The walk ends when the apex is a. After the walk it locates a as a vertex into (T, s), swaps a and b, and loops. The flip 0x004C9FC0 it reaches through the sweep moves bits only between edges that carry them; the whole-map build therefore carries none (ADR-0461). Built as `NavMesh.InsertConstraint` in the original's slots (NavMesh.Slots.cs, ADR-0486), replacing the Sloan sweep of ADR-0157; with it the whole-map build of the Level 10 bytes matches DK2's first dump in 2,826 of 2,828 triangles and 2,823 slots, and the per-tile rebuild reproduces every later dump.
