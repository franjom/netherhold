Takes fixed-point position and angle pointers (two stack arguments, `RET 8`). Before branching on thing class, writes the supplied position through the position setter and relinks the thing to its tile; it does not snap x/y to a tile centre. For a creature it clears `+0x3E6` bit 0, stamps `+0x4BF` with the current turn and calls SetState(76) at `0x004BC7CD`-`0x004BC7D7` (76, not 77; 77 is the slap's). For an object dropped on a live creature with non-zero level-scaled Pay or HungerFill it stores that creature's id as the earmark (`0x004BC92B`, `0x004BC994`); ownership, debt, hunger and distance are not tested. It also reveals fog, and the keeper AI drops through it with no tile. The 76 entry is marked STRONGLY INFERRED in hand-drop-landing.md, as is the earmark rule. Built in HandSystem, DropLandingRules and DropDeliveryRules (ADR-0226).

Sounds (read 2026-11-02): a creature ends on positional 0xC4 (0x004BC81D) in its category (+0x6E3 of its definition) with handle 0 at the supplied position, after the state kick to 76 and the position write; an object (class 2) ends on 0xC4 (0x004BCA02) in its definition's category (+0x10E) with its handle +0x2E at its own position +0x1A, after the gold effect. Neither is conditional on the destination, so every drop sounds, the AI keepers' too. Only object_gold has C4 samples among the objects. Built in HandSystem.Drop and DropObject, after the drop gate.

Position (traced 2026-11-02). The human drop 0x004C0EE0 builds the position with its height zero-initialised at 0x004C0F0B and never set; X and Y come from the creature at keeper +0x196 (its +0x1A..+0x22, height included) when that is live, else from keeper +0x18E/+0x192. It calls this function at 0x004C0F70, and the C4 at 0x004BC81D uses that pointer. Built in HandSystem.DropPoint: a floor drop sounds at the pointer with height 0, a drop onto a creature at that creature's position. Deviation: the keeper AI drops with its own position (it has no human path) and ours takes the pointer from the command, the tile centre when it has none.
