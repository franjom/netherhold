Takes a fixed-point position and a target (two stack arguments, `RET 8`). For a creature it clears `+0x3E6` bit 0, stamps `+0x4BF` with the current turn and calls SetState(76) at `0x004BC7CD`-`0x004BC7D7` (76, not 77; 77 is the slap's). For an object dropped on a live creature with non-zero level-scaled Pay or HungerFill it stores that creature's id as the earmark (`0x004BC92B`, `0x004BC994`); ownership, debt, hunger and distance are not tested. It also reveals fog, and the keeper AI drops through it with no tile. The 76 entry is marked CONFIRMED in hand-drop-landing.md; the earmark rule is STRONGLY INFERRED. Built in HandSystem, DropLandingRules and DropDeliveryRules (ADR-0226).
