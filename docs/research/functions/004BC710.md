Takes fixed-point position and angle pointers (two stack arguments, `RET 8`). Before branching on thing class, writes the supplied position through the position setter and relinks the thing to its tile; it does not snap x/y to a tile centre. For a creature it clears `+0x3E6` bit 0, stamps `+0x4BF` with the current turn and calls SetState(76) at `0x004BC7CD`-`0x004BC7D7` (76, not 77; 77 is the slap's). For an object dropped on a live creature with non-zero level-scaled Pay or HungerFill it stores that creature's id as the earmark (`0x004BC92B`, `0x004BC994`); ownership, debt, hunger and distance are not tested. It also reveals fog, and the keeper AI drops through it with no tile. The 76 entry is marked STRONGLY INFERRED in hand-drop-landing.md, as is the earmark rule. Built in HandSystem, DropLandingRules and DropDeliveryRules (ADR-0226).

Sounds (read 2026-11-02): a creature ends on positional 0xC4 (0x004BC81D) in its category (+0x6E3 of its definition) with handle 0 at the supplied position, after the state kick to 76 and the position write; an object (class 2) ends on 0xC4 (0x004BCA02) in its definition's category (+0x10E) with its handle +0x2E at its own position +0x1A, after the gold effect. Neither is conditional on the destination, so every drop sounds, the AI keepers' too. Only object_gold has C4 samples among the objects. Built in HandSystem.Drop and DropObject, after the drop gate.

Position (traced 2026-11-02). The human drop 0x004C0EE0 builds the position with X and Y from the creature at keeper +0x196 when that is live, else from keeper +0x18E/+0x192; on both arms it then writes the height 0x2000 (MOV [ESP+0x14] at 0x004C0F68, after the two pushes, so the slot zeroed at 0x004C0F0B). It calls this function at 0x004C0F70, and the C4 at 0x004BC81D uses that pointer. Built in HandSystem.DropPoint: a drop sounds at the pointer, or at the X and Y of the creature under the hand, with height 0x2000. The computer keeper's callers pass x and y as the tile's centre plus 0x100, height 0 and angle 0 (read 2026-11-10, STRONGLY INFERRED): 0x004FF589 in ComputerPlayer_RetreatWounded, 0x004FFA1A in ComputerPlayer_TrainingMoves and 0x004F95F4 in ComputerPlayer_DisposeCreature, the last two placed by address between record starts. Deviation: ours takes the pointer from the command (the tile's centre when it has none), keeps the heading for a command without an angle and gives every drop the human one-turn fall.

Creature arm in order (read 2026-11-10, STRONGLY INFERRED): the position set through 0x004B5560 (0x004BC751); SetState(76) through 0x0048AD30 (0x004BC7D7); the point copied into +0xF6/+0xFA/+0xFE; Body_StopAtOwnPosition 0x004D1070 (0x004BC7FB), which routes the body to its own position and zeroes its velocity; the sound; then a jump to the tail at 0x004BCA08..0x004BCA19, which writes the angle word to +0xF4 and +0x102 with no masking. The stop precedes the angle write, so no turn happens inside this function. It runs as hand state 4's enter callback (0x004C0EE0) inside the packet dispatch (call at 0x0050A527), before the entity sweep at 0x0050A7D7.
