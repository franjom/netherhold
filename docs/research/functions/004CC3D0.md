Arguments (x, y) in tiles. Locates (x << 8, y << 8) with 0x004C98B0; returns at once on -1. Pushes onto the flood stack 0x006E6F48 (count 0x006ECA54), stamping each with the generation byte 0x006A11E8, every triangle that is live (+0xC != 0xFF), not yet stamped and not solid (bit 3 of +0xC clear), in this order: the located triangle, then its neighbours in slots 0, 1, 2 (+6, +8, +0xA). Then 0x004C9B50 finds the vertex slot of the corner in the located triangle; if the corner is a vertex, it walks the fan from the neighbour across that slot, at each triangle finding the slot that points back to the previous one and stepping on through the next slot (table 0x0066DA14 = [1, 2, 0]), pushing each triangle under the same test, and stops on returning to the located triangle or at a -1 neighbour. Called four times by 0x004CC650, once per corner. The fan triangles already pushed as neighbours are skipped by the stamp, so the push order is: corner triangle, its neighbours, the rest of the fan in walk order.
