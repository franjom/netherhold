Called from `0x00468520` for kinds 1 and 2. Tasks are ranked by squared distance to the task tile's centre, from the imp, or from the last task tile `+0x8C/+0x90` after a mine (`+0x98` = 2); a later task replaces the best only when strictly nearer, free or held. Within a task the four sides are tried from the one `0x004805F0` picks (facing the imp, or the last seam), each side three slots (`0x00698A80`: 682 in from the wall edge, at 682/2047/3412 along it). A side counts when it is not Solid, passes `0x00471630` with flag 0x80. A free slot ends the task's search at once; a held one whose holder passes the shared holder test (live creature, state machine, not 51/52, `0x00477310(10)`) is a fallback, the last such winning: a dig when the imp's distance to the wall's centre is below the holder's to the side tile's centre, a mine when the imp's distance from where it stands to the side tile's corner is below the holder's to that corner. A seam also refuses a slot holding a gold pile over `[0x006F25CD]`; nothing refuses a slot for a creature standing in it. The holder is kicked with `0x0048AD30(10)` before the walk; a dead or reused holder id counts as free. Writes `+0x98` 1 and state 13 for a wall, 2 and state 25 for a seam (imp-task-stealing.md §2, §9).
