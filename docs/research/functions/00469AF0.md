A task qualifies when a side (tried from the one facing the imp) is not Solid, passes `0x00471630` with flag 0x80 and has a free holder slot of three (`0x00508110`) or a stealable one; seams also refuse a slot while a creature stands in its third of the face. After a mining job (`+0x98` = 2) seams are measured from the last task tile `+0x8C/+0x90`.
