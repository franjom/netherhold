Called on the object with one signed amount. It adds the amount to +0x14A, the union's first dword that the object dump names GoldHeld, and clamps a negative result to zero. For type ids 1 and 2 (+0x12C) it then rewrites the scale fields +0x48/+0x4C and zeroes +0x58/+0x5C: a fixed scale at 5000 or more, otherwise one computed from the amount. It returns the new GoldHeld. Every taker passes a negated amount and destroys the object (vtable +0x20) when zero comes back: the walk-over pick-up in 0x00488930 (three sites), the possessed pick-up in 0x00514830, the imp's state 27 at 0x004669BE, the room ring 0x004F4510, and the deposit and store routines 0x004F3C60 and 0x004F4090. Player_ChangeGold, the Hand pick-up, the floor put-down 0x004510C0 and 0x0049EF90 also call it. The exact scale formula was not read. Ours keeps the amount on ObjectState.GoldHeld and has no scale.
