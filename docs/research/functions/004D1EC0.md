thiscall: ECX the vector, then (out*, k) on the stack. Each component goes into EDX:EAX as itself shifted up 16 (SHL EAX 16, SAR EDX 16) and is IDIV'd by k. No globals.

Our Fixed16.Div wraps where the original's IDIV faults (on a zero scalar our C# throws, as the original faults). DK2 itself would crash on those inputs, so they are outside what can be matched. Everywhere else Fixed16Vector.Over matches the emulated original, on 31944 inputs, of which 12056 fault (tests/DK2.Oracle.Tests/OriginalFixed16Tests.cs).
