The only reader of `def+0x5C6` (MeleeRecharge, a dword the loader has already turned into whole ticks) outside the copier; it re-promotes the value to 12-bit fixed point (`SHL 12`) and has 6 callers. Like 0x00494D00 it is called on the definition with the creature as argument, and it folds `+0x3E6` bit 7 (ceiling 100) and `+0x3EA` bit 3 (ceiling 200) against global `+0x351`, plus `+0x460` in the first arm. Its best-read caller is the Gate B predicate 0x0048C9B0, which divides the result by the work rate at `+0x3AF`/`+0x3B3` and compares against the stamp at `+0x427`; combat-cadence.md §4 is explicit that the melee swing itself waits on `def+0x5CA` instead. Read from the binary but the possession arms are not independently confirmed (Q393). Not built as such: `CombatRules.SwingMayBegin` uses the plain tick conversion of MeleeRecharge, i.e. only this function's no-flag arm, and says the modifiers are not modelled.
