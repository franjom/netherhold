About 130 guarded copies, each skipped when its presence dword is zero; the staging frame sits at file offset plus 0x3F0, confirmed at both ends of the record and by the opening byte-sum of 0x1939 bytes into def+0x5AF (0x1939 minus 0x3F0 is the record size). Examples used across the docs: Pay to def+0x69A, the melee block to def+0x5C0..0x5CA, speeds divided by variable 19 into def+0x5B4..0x5BC, resistances packed into def+0x61C with a count at def+0x78A (combat.md §6.1.1). Direct calls are confirmed; combat-cadence.md §5 records their sites and the remaining reachability limit. Combat.md §6.1.1 records that an earlier reading took it for a dumper. Our KwdCreatures parser reads the file record directly rather than mirroring this copy step, so it is READ.
