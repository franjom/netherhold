A place spell: the hovered tile's centre must be in the map (slot +0x200, 0x00509050); a tile of a Temple (room type byte +0x18 == 13) whose interior byte +0xC is 0xFF is accepted at once, returning before the rest; otherwise slot +0x254 (0x0050E530, the position permission 0x004BB8A0) decides. A thing spell, by hover kind +0x1084: 2 and 3 (creatures) ask slot +0x258 (0x004BBA40), and spell 10 is also refused when the creature's +0x40 equals its level-scaled maximum (def +0x692, table 0x006E4154 + 0xE1); 4 (an object) asks slot +0x25C (0x004BBBC0); 1 (a tile) passes only on the Temple interior; any other kind is invalid.
