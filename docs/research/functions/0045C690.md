Used by fleeing state 203 (0x004602E0). It walks the creature's melee slots, then its ranged slots, and keeps the lowest distance. Distance across alignments is cut by an eighth and the Creeping percentage is applied. A candidate must be IsAttackable, must not be the creature's +0x4A3, must be seen within sight range or heard within hearing range (an Imp sees one tile and hears nothing), and must be routable. combat-target-selection.md lists it as one of the few readers of the creeping flag, all of them target scoring, and combat-fear.md marks every step of section 4 CONFIRMED (binary). Ours mirrors it as FearRules.NearestAttacker.
