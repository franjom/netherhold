Reads the tile's thing chain and selects the first object of type 1 whose x/y
Chebyshev distance from the supplied point is strictly below 0x40. Adds the
entire supplied amount without a pile-size cap. Otherwise creates neutral
object 1 at that point and adds the amount. Unlike fall-and-land, this helper
does not check candidate health or third-of-tile bands.

The mining caller 0x004971D0 supplies its own position and the gold that does
not fit in its purse. Our PutDown retains the existing cell approximation;
repeated overflow at one mining slot accumulates into one pile.
