Visits the four orthogonal neighbours in the order N, E, S, W (0x006BC350), tests the neighbour's room word and calls Room_Rebuild (0x004ECE78) when Room_HasPillars (0x004E4300; true for the Hatchery) holds or the definition's +0x43E bit 0 is set. Callers: Tile_ApplyWork 0x00453221 (zero health, destroyed type differs from the current, after Tile_SetTerrain or Player_SellRoomTile), 0x004533C0 (maximum health, maximum type differs, after Tile_SetTerrain) and Imp_DigAction 0x0049716B (the applier returned 0), so a wall dug beside a Hatchery rebuilds it twice. The Hatchery arm is built: HatcheryRules.AfterNeighbourWork, called from TileWorkSystem after a terrain change from work and after a dig kind's zero, and from TremorRules when a wall falls (ADR-0672). The Training Room SpecialWalls arm is built at437 through the same neighbor loop and TrainingFurnitureRules.Rebuild (ADR-0705); remaining room arms stay partial. This is not the ownership-loss wall-support callback.
