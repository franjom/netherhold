One of the five arms that SelectAVictim (0x0045CB00) dispatches on FightStyle; at 0x7DC bytes it is nearly three times any other arm and is the only one with a heart test and hero handling. It is also the heaviest writer of the target field creature+0x502 (12 of 43 stores). Near its own heart it judges hostiles by the shared distance template; otherwise it can send the worker to respond to a threat (travel call 0x00475448, state 251), and after its head-count a failure restores the target (combat-fear.md). combat-alarm.md section 1 marks the arm CONFIRMED (binary); combat-target-selection.md calls "it is the non-combatant rule" strongly inferred, from its dispatch and its two creatures. Ours mirrors it in CombatTargetRules.
