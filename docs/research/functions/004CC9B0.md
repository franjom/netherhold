The fill step of 0x004CCCB0. Calls 0x004C9C30 four times, once per corner, each returning the triangle whose edge leaves that corner toward the next; stamps each distinct one (byte at +0xC) and sums their twice-areas through 0x004CC120, returning when the sum equals `(x1 - x0) * (y1 - y0) * 2` (a rectangle of one or two triangles). Otherwise 0x004CC850 sweeps every slot from `DAT_006ECA2C - 1` down to 0, stamping each live triangle whose three vertices lie in the box until the sum is reached. There is no neighbour walk. Ours (`NavMesh.InsertRectangle`) stamps the same set, the triangles with every corner inside the rectangle, by a flood from one of them, with no corner fast path and no area exit; nothing observable differs, since no slot is allocated and the set is the same.
