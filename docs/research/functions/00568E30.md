Two reads pin the +0x3F0 mapping against instructions: the four 2-byte resistance pairs from file +0xE0C into record +0x11FC (at 0x0056983E), and the base-type bytes record +0xD3B and +0xF4B, which land in staging +0x112B and +0x133B before the builder moves them to def+0x5B3 and def+0x770.
