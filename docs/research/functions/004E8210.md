Receiver ECX is the room; four stack arguments (RET 0x10): take floor slots, take wall slots, out position (three dwords), out byte. Returns 0 at once when the tile at the room's FirstSlab (+0x2D/+0x2E) has no room id (word +2 & 0xFFF). Walks the chain (tile +0x04 is the next slab) counting tiles Room_IsFloorSlot 0x00454110 accepts (first flag) or 0x00454230 accepts (second flag); none returns 0 without a draw. Otherwise draws random(count) from the world stream (world+0x15A0, source line 0xF42), walks to that index and writes (x x 0x1000 + 0x800, y x 0x1000 + 0x800, 0); a wall slot also copies the byte 0x00454230 returned through its out argument (meaning unread). Callers include the training moves 0x004FF5C0 (1, 0) and the prisoner step's Torture move 0x005006D0 (1, 1).
