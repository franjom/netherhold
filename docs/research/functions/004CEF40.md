A zero last-index corridor writes the destination as one waypoint. Other corridors tighten the portal funnel and emit corner points as needed, then the destination. Result +0x10 is the count and points begin at +0x14 as x/y dword pairs. Combat later sums these legs with its own octile metric; this routine has no door-thing substitution.
