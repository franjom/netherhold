Takes a creature and a delta. On reaching the threshold it zeroes the balance instead of subtracting it, so the overshoot is lost and one large award cannot gain several levels (Q127, ADR-0083); a zero threshold refuses every change, and a loss larger than the balance does nothing. It also refuses every change for a Combat Pit fighter at or above CombatPitMaxExperienceLevel (combat-pit.md section 7). training.md section 7 goes through it clause by clause and is marked CONFIRMED. Ours mirrors it in CreatureLevelRules and the pit cap in CombatPitRules.AtTheCap. combat.md section 7 leaves its combat callers (experience from a kill) open.
