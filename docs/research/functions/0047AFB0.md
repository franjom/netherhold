Calls the world's +0x1D4 for the cell under the creature (+0x1A); a cell with no thing (low 12 bits zero) or a room 0x004E7F80 refuses returns 0, so SetState leaves the creature in state 1 with no message. Otherwise returns 0x004E5A70's answer. The other claiming enter hooks make the same lookup; the Spectating, Combat Pit and Manufacture hooks (0x0047A9E0, 0x0047AAE0, 0x0047B0A0) do their own step first, and the Casino and Training hooks (0x0047ACB0, 0x0047B230) can refuse before it. Dispatched by CategoryHooks as RoomCapacityRules.Claim (ADR-0309).
