Called once per player by the per-player update `0x004C2310`. At `0x004B9F57` it rebuilds `player+0x7E` (gold) as the sum of `+0x49` over the player's rooms of type 1 (Treasury) and type 5 (Heart, when its `+0x9A` bit 0 is clear); that is a drift correction, since `0x004BA420` keeps the total live. It returns early for the neutral tag and the hero tag (setting hero mana to 0x7FFFFFFF), so the mana update through `0x004BB2A0`/`0x004C10E0` and the decay call `0x004C3340` are keepers' alone; it calls the AI `0x00500C20` when the computer-control byte is set. Read from the binary in several docs that agree. Not mirrored as one routine; ManaSystem copies its keeper-only gate.
