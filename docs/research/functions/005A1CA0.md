Reads the 4x4 block of tile height nibbles (tile word +2 >> 12) around the tile (out-of-bounds tiles count 0), then makes 9 heights z = -2 - n/4 + n*n/128 where n is the bilinear mix of the nibbles at the tile (0,0), east (1,0), south (0,1) and south-east (1,1) at vertex (row r, column c) of the 3x3 grid; the centre and each edge midpoint whose neighbour tile is not solid at its centre (world slot +0x218) are then raised to at most z = -2.5. Four corner normals come from height differences with a constant z component of 4.0, are bilinearly spread over the 3x3 (0x005A18A0) and normalised (their exact pairing is UNREAD). Colour bytes (0,0,0) become three floats /255 and are the instance tint.
