Otherwise it looks the thing up in the id table 0x006EE540, calls 0x004B4F60 on it at 0x0048B373 (off its tile chain), stores the id at +0x3C3, and when the thing's dword +0x12 is 2 calls the thing's vtable +0x78 (not read); returns 1. Its drop twin is 0x0048B3A0. Callers: 0x0049ADA3 (in 0x0049AC80), 0x0048290B and 0x004829A6 (in 0x00482880), 0x00482CF3 (in 0x00482C50); the last two functions are unread. CarryRules.PickUp and ObjectCarryRules.PickUp set the carried ids, whose setters take the thing off the chain (ThingTileLink.None); the already-carrying refusal is left to their callers, and the vtable +0x78 call is not mirrored.
