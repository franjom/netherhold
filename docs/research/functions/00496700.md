It writes the parameter dwords of the block at `creature+0x38D`-`+0x3AE`, the index at `+0x3A9`, the start stamp `+0x3A5` from the game turn, clears `+0x3AD`/`+0x3AE`, and sets `creature+0x3EA` bit 12 (PlayInstanceAnimation). Three thin wrappers at 0x00496980, 0x004969D0 and 0x00496A30 call it and copy two or three extra dwords into `+0x38D`; across the 109 call sites of it and its wrappers, 105 push a literal index, which is the source of the who-starts-what map. Melee combat is action 17 (started only from the possession handler at 0x00514DA4 and spFighting at 0x00460103), spells action 16. It is one of the four `+0x3A5` writers inside 0x00496700-0x00496D80 (combat-cadence.md §4), which is why the swing's Gate A counts from the start of the action. creature-action-table.md marks it CONFIRMED (binary); src/ has no general action layer mirroring it.
