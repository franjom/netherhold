before insertion. Thing +4 is the id word; +0x0A/+0x0C are previous/next
id words; +0x16 is the slot dword. The new next is the old tile-slot head,
the old head's previous becomes the new id, and the tile-slot head becomes
that id. Its head address is world +0x9EE + tileIndex*16 + slot*2; the
address stride does not establish eight independent chain heads per tile.
The trailing 0x004B5020 call receives the thing's current position.
