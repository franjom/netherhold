A thiscall on world `+0x08` (the thing lists) with the door type, owner tag, position, orientation and an out pointer. It refuses when the door definition (slot `+0x134` of `[this+0x14]`) is missing or no thing slot is free. The twin of `World_CreateTrap` `0x004B7C10`.
