`__thiscall` on a camera object embedded at `+0x3E5` of the view that calls it (0x0043BFD5), with one argument; the same object is what the view's vtable slot 0xD0 returns to trigger condition 72 and to the possession step 0x0040E910. The mode is `+0xDA8`. Mode 1 copies the followed entity's position; mode 2 follows the possessed creature (`+0xDEC`) with its head bob and sway; mode 0x12 steps a frame counter against `+0xEBE` and ends in 0x0044D8F0, a scripted path; mode 0xB times out 2000 ms after its start. In every mode the real-time clock drives straight-line blends: position toward a target until `+0xDFA` (optionally tracking an entity at `+0xE02`), yaw from `+0xE1C` toward `+0xE1E` until `+0xE20`, `+0xD80` until `+0xE14`, `+0xD8E` until `+0xE2C`, and the full position, angle and zoom transition until `+0xDD8`. Each deadline is zeroed when reached. When the mode pauses them (modes 2, 7, 0xB) they are pushed later by the time elapsed, so they do not expire.

Mode 0x12 (instructions 0x0044C1EE..0x0044C284): elapsed real milliseconds since +0xECE times 0.03 (0x0066D1A0), whole frames added to +0xEC6, the fraction accumulated in +0xECA with a carry at 1.0; when +0xEC6 >= +0xEBE - 1 it calls 0x0044D8F0. Camera paths therefore play at 30 frames per wall-clock second (camera-paths.md §3). The letterbox blend (+0xE4A start, +0xE4E duration, +0xE52 from, +0xE72 to, +0xE62 current) is stepped at the top of the function.

So the four fields condition 72 tests are the camera's in-flight moves, not modal dialogs: the condition is true when the camera is still. Read in full except the helpers each mode calls; LIKELY. CameraPathRules models path duration in nominal turns (ADR-0496/0498); condition 72 waits for that duration, while unmodelled move deadlines remain assumed still.
