Arguments (x, y) in 1/256 tile. The cell is `clamp(x >> 14, 0, 3) + 4 * clamp(y >> 14, 0, 3)` (64-tile squares of the canvas); the start is `DAT_006E5AC0[cell]`, refreshed through 0x004C97B0 when its byte at +0xC is 0xFF (dead). Each step computes the three 64-bit orientation signs of the point against the edges 0, 1, 2 and switches on `(s0 > 0) + 2 * (s1 > 0) + 4 * (s2 > 0)`: 0 stores the triangle in the cache and returns it; 1, 2, 4 step across that one edge's neighbour word (+6, +8, +10), unguarded against -1; 3, 5, 6 and 7 turn the fan through 0x004C9540 around the corner the two edges share (slot 1, 0, 2, 2). A point on an edge or at a vertex therefore returns the first closed-containing triangle the walk reaches, which depends on what was located before in that cell by this routine's callers: 0x004C9B50 (vertex locate), 0x004CAA50 (point insertion), 0x004CC3D0 (flood seed) and 0x004C9DF0 (route endpoint). Built as `NavMesh.Locate` with the cache as state, saved and hashed (ADR-0486); on the Level 10 capture the per-tile rebuild then reproduces DK2's dumps slot for slot.
