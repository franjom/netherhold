Starts at the cache entry `DAT_006E5AC0[min(max(x >> 14, 0), 3) + 4 * clamp(y >> 14)]`; if the cached triangle's byte is 0xFF (dead) the entry is refreshed through 0x004C97B0. At each step it computes the three 64-bit orientation signs of the point against the triangle's edges and switches on them: no sign > 0 returns the current triangle and stores it in the cache; one sign > 0 steps across that edge; cases 3, 5, 6 and 7 (beyond two edges) turn the fan through 0x004C9540. A point on an edge or at a vertex therefore returns the first closed-containing triangle the walk reaches, which depends on what was located before. Ours (`NavMesh.Locate`, ADR-0157) walks from one hint and then returns the lowest slot holding the point, so the fan triangle it names at a vertex can differ from DK2's; that triangle starts the degree reduction and labels the degree-4 rim in the per-tile rebuild, and heads the flood seed's stack (0x004CC3D0 locates first), a residual stated in ADR-0461. Callers include the vertex locate 0x004C9B50, the endpoint locate 0x004C9DF0, the flood seed 0x004CC3D0 and the point insertion.
