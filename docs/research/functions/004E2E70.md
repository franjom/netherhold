Arguments are (message, player, all): over the room's floor tiles, each thing on the ground and not dying whose owner is player (or any, with all) is sent to a random point in the tile south of the door, x first, with route options 0x68 when 0x0048EBF0 accepts the tile and 0x6C otherwise, or with no door to a rim tile from 0x004E8430, resuming 121. With message set it posts 0x42 to the room's owner, and it calls 0x004E2D40(1) once per creature released (on the no-door path only when a rim tile is found). Callers: state 122 (the rescuer's side, (1, owner, 0)), the wrapper 0x004E2E60 = (1, 0, 1) used by the claim and the overcrowding break, and 0x004E2C30 (0, room owner, 0). jail-break.md §5 marks it CONFIRMED and the 2026-09-26 verification re-read it; that verification also found ours sets the broken flag even when nobody is freed (inert). prison.md's shorter account ("released at the door tile with an effect") predates the full reading.
