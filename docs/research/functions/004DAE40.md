The verdict tail calls it every turn a killer is stamped, in place of the general arm 0x004DAA10, when the mode byte 0x0075AD4B is 1 (single player) and the player is local. On the death turn only the Portal (3), Wooden Bridge (8), Stone Bridge (17) and Hero Stone Bridge (22) go neutral; every other room has its slab things removed (tile bit 14 cleared) and gets the heart obstacle object. From 15 s + 1 turn after death the non-heart rooms' slabs become their destroyed terrain (0x0044FC40) and the rooms are freed (0x004ECD00), a Destructor (object 98) is made at the heart, and then one more on a random owned tile whenever none lives (player+0xB4C) until player+0x76 bit 14 is set; its creatures leave from the death turn, not the fifth. Read from the binary in win-and-defeat.md §7; DECISIONS.md (ADR-0162 amendment) and DefeatRules.cs mark it research only, so READ. It is also one of the 40 callers of the health routine (combat.md §1) and one of the clearers of the tile movement bit +8 0x4000 (fog-reveal-sources.md §1).
