It sits at slot +0x01 of the handler records of both room 3 (Portal) and room 25 (Hero Portal), which are byte-identical (portals-mercenary.md section 1); the generic room constructor 0x004E3800 reaches it through that slot. Re-read in the binary on 2026-09-30, it is three stores: room+0x49 = the game turn from 0x00517490, room+0x4D = 0, room+0xA3 = 1. The ownership-change handler 0x004DBBC0 right after it makes the same first two stores and not the third, so ADR-0086 amendment 1 was right that both stamp the clock, and portals-interval.md, which said only the ownership change does, is corrected. What room+0xA3 means is unread. Ours has no constructor of its own, but its effect is matched: a new portal's centre tile starts with EntranceCreaturesGenerated and EntranceIdleTicks both 0, which is the same as a zero tally and a clock stamped now (PortalRules.OnOwnerChanged resets the same pair on a change of hands). LIKELY: read statically.
