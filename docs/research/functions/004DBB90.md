It sits at slot +0x01 of the handler records of both room 3 (Portal) and room 25 (Hero Portal), which are byte-identical (portals-mercenary.md §1); the generic room constructor 0x004E3800 reaches it through that slot. It is one of the two places that store zero into the per-room tally room+0x4D that sets the arrival interval, the other being the ownership-change handler 0x004DBBC0. The docs disagree on one detail: portals-interval.md says only the ownership-change handler also stamps room+0x49 = now, while DECISIONS.md (ADR-0086 amendment 1) says both handlers stamp the clock and zero the tally; the handler table is CONFIRMED but this slot's body is described only by its stores, hence DISPUTED until reread. Not built as code of its own: ours simply starts a new portal's tally at zero (PortalRules.cs notes a fresh portal starts at zero).
