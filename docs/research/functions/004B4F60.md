When slot +0x16 is not 2, splices the previous/next id words at +0x0A/+0x0C:
a head removal replaces the tile-slot head, otherwise the previous thing's
next is replaced. A nonzero next gets the old previous. Both own links are
then zeroed, and the slot dword becomes 2. Whole body and instructions
through 0x004B5016 checked. The carry pick-up 0x0048B350 calls it (0x0048B373)
before storing the carried id at creature +0x3C3.
