Thiscall on the flower renderer with the creature. The six-pass loop re-runs the same lookup each time (only the creature is passed), so it is one test. The viewing player comes from this+4 vtable +0xBC and its Number from player +0x10FC. It copies 0x1B bytes of the chosen resource to this+0xED, installs it (Animation_InstallResource_LIKELY) and draws it with Thing_DrawRenderInfo. Entries 6 and 10 of the +0x124 icon table (pointers at 0x006BE580) are Objective and Objective-2.
