Creature_LevelUp returns the level byte +0x403 and changes nothing when the level is already 10 or creature vtable +0x28 (Thing_IsDead 0x004B5C30: health +0x40 at or below 0) is true. So a creature below level 10 with health at or below 0 on the list makes this loop spin for ever and hangs the game; whether such a creature can be on the list when the action runs is unread.
